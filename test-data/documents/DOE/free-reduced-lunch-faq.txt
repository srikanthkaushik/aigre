DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Free & Reduced Lunch Program FAQ
Last updated: September 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I apply for free or reduced-price school lunch for my child?

A: Apply through the portal under "DOE — Meal Program" or via the paper
form sent home at the start of the school year. Eligibility follows
federal income guidelines administered through DOE Nutrition Services —
this is a federally-funded, school-administered program, NOT the same as
DHHS city-administered emergency assistance benefits, and does not affect
or require enrollment in any DHHS program.

Q: My application was denied. Can I appeal the same way as a DHHS
benefits appeal?

A: No — meal-program appeals follow the federal program's own review
process (a written request to DOE Nutrition Services within 10 business
days), not the DHHS Benefits Appeals Policy or the Citywide Grievance
Appeals Process. Different program, different appeal path, even though
both involve "benefits" in casual conversation.

Q: My child says they weren't given a meal despite being approved. What
do I do?

A: Report through the portal under "DOE — Meal Program" as a service
complaint (distinct from the eligibility-application process above) —
reviewed within 3 business days given the immediacy of a child going
without a meal.

Q: Is this connected to DHHS's food safety inspections of school
cafeterias?

A: No — DHHS Food Safety Complaint Investigation SOP covers licensed
food-service sanitation, a completely different concern from meal-program
eligibility or service delivery.
