DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Student Safety & Anti-Bullying Policy
Document No. DOE-POL-004           Effective Date: September 1, 2023
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DOE's investigation of bullying, harassment, and student-safety
complaints submitted through the citizen grievance portal, consistent
with State Education Code §48900 et seq.

2. JURISDICTION — ON AND OFF SCHOOL GROUNDS

  2.1  DOE has jurisdiction to investigate bullying complaints occurring
       on school grounds, during school-sponsored activities or
       transportation, and — critically — off-campus conduct that
       creates a substantial disruption to the school environment or
       involves students from the same school (e.g., cyberbullying
       between classmates occurring after school hours). Jurisdiction
       does NOT require the incident to have physically occurred on
       school property.

  2.2  Purely private disputes between students unrelated to the school
       environment (e.g., a dispute at a private party with no school
       connection) are outside DOE's investigative jurisdiction and are
       referred to families and, where applicable, law enforcement.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  Reports describing physical safety risk (threats of violence,
       weapons) are classified CRITICAL and referred immediately to the
       school principal and DOE Safety Office, with same-day response.

  3.2  Reports of ongoing harassment/bullying without an immediate
       physical-safety element are classified MEDIUM priority;
       investigation opened within 5 business days per state-mandated
       timelines.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide for concerns that
rise to the level of suspected abuse or neglect (a different, broader
process than a school-bullying investigation), and DOE School Health
Services Guide for concerns about a school nurse or student medical care,
which is a distinct process from a safety/bullying investigation even
though both fall under DOE.
