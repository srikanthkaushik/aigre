DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
School Facilities Accessibility (ADA) Policy
Document No. DOE-POL-016           Effective Date: April 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints about physical accessibility barriers at DOE facilities
for students, staff, or visitors with disabilities — ramps, accessible
restrooms, elevators in multi-story school buildings. [[XREF]]Distinct from a
special-education SERVICES/curriculum accommodation, which is governed by
DOE Special Education Services FAQ under IDEA rather than physical ADA
compliance.[[/XREF]]

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  BROKEN ACCESSIBILITY EQUIPMENT (elevator, automatic door) AFFECTING
       CURRENT ACCESS: HIGH priority, [[XREF]]same standard as an elevator outage
       in DHUD public housing[[/XREF]] — repair dispatched same day, alternate
       accommodation (temporary route, staff assistance) arranged
       immediately if repair will take longer than a school day.

  2.2  STRUCTURAL BARRIER REQUIRING CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT (no ramp at a
       building entrance): Logged for the DOE capital-improvement
       planning process, not a standard repair SLA — but an interim
       accommodation must still be arranged immediately for any affected
       individual.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOE Special Education Services FAQ (curriculum/services
accommodation, a different kind of accommodation) and DOE School
Facilities Maintenance SOP.
