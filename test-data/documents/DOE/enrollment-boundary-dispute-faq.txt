DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
School Enrollment Boundary Dispute FAQ
Last updated: May 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: I think my address was assigned to the wrong school boundary. How do I
dispute it?

A: Submit a boundary review request through the portal under "DOE —
Enrollment Boundary." This is reviewed against the official boundary map
within 15 business days — not a standard grievance SLA, since it requires
a formal geographic determination rather than a service-repair timeline.

Q: Can I request a transfer to a different school outside my boundary for
non-boundary-dispute reasons (preference, sibling, etc.)?

A: Yes, but that's a separate School Choice Transfer application process
with its own annual enrollment window, not a boundary dispute and not
handled through this FAQ's process.

Q: My boundary dispute was denied. Can I appeal?

A: Yes, through the Citywide Grievance Appeals Process, reviewed on the
boundary-map evidence.

Q: Is a dispute about which school my child was assigned to the same as a
complaint about a specific teacher or classroom?

A: No — boundary/assignment disputes are administrative determinations.
Complaints about a specific teacher's conduct are handled under DOE
Teacher Conduct Complaint Policy, an entirely different process.
