DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
School Facilities Maintenance SOP
Document No. DOE-SOP-008           Effective Date: June 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DOE Facilities Division's response to citizen and staff reports
of maintenance issues in public school buildings: HVAC, plumbing,
electrical, roofing, and structural concerns.

2. SCOPE

Applies to DOE-owned and operated school buildings. Does not apply to
sidewalks or streets adjacent to a school (see DOT/DPW policies for
right-of-way infrastructure) or to a school's playground perimeter fence
specifically bordering a public street, which is a shared-jurisdiction
item under Section 4.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  HVAC FAILURE affecting an occupied classroom (no heat/cooling
       during a session day): HIGH priority, technician dispatched same
       day, target restoration within 24 hours given student-safety
       implications of extreme temperatures.

  3.2  PLUMBING / ELECTRICAL (non-hazardous): MEDIUM priority, repair
       within 5 business days.

  3.3  STRUCTURAL / HAZARD (exposed wiring, ceiling damage, broken
       glass in an occupied area): CRITICAL priority per the Citywide
       Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy, area cordoned off immediately,
       repair dispatched same day.

4. PLAYGROUND FENCING AND STREET-ADJACENT HAZARDS

Where a damaged playground fence or gate borders a public street and
creates a risk of a child entering traffic, DOE Facilities coordinates
same-day emergency temporary fencing while DOT/DPW are separately engaged
for any street-side hazard (e.g., traffic calming, signage). Do not treat
this as solely a DOE matter or solely a DOT/DPW matter — both apply, and
the case should be dual-tagged.

5. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy, DOT Traffic Signal
Repair & Timing Policy (school-zone provisions), and DPW Sidewalk
Maintenance Policy.
