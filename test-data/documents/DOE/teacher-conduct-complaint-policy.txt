DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Teacher Conduct Complaint Policy
Document No. DOE-POL-014           Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints about a teacher's or staff member's own conduct toward
a student — verbal demeaning behavior, unfair treatment, or inappropriate
discipline. [[XREF]]This is DISTINCT from DOE Student Safety & Anti-Bullying
Policy, which covers PEER-TO-PEER conduct between students. A complaint
about an adult staff member's behavior toward a student always goes
through this policy, not the anti-bullying policy, even though both
involve a student feeling mistreated at school.[[/XREF]]

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  ALLEGATION OF PHYSICAL CONTACT OR SEVERE VERBAL ABUSE: CRITICAL
       priority per the Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy,
       immediate reassignment consideration pending investigation,
       cross-referenced against DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation
       Guide if the allegation meets that threshold.

  2.2  PATTERN OF DEMEANING OR UNFAIR TREATMENT (verbal, not physical):
       MEDIUM priority, investigation opened within 5 business days,
       consistent with state-mandated timelines for personnel-conduct
       investigations involving students.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOE Student Safety & Anti-Bullying Policy (peer conduct, not
staff conduct) and DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide.
