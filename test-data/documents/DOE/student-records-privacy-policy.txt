DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Student Records Privacy Policy
Document No. DOE-POL-019           Effective Date: June 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs how student education records are protected when a grievance
involves a specific student — consistent with the federal Family
Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Citywide Privacy & PII
Handling Notice's baseline protections, with additional FERPA-specific
requirements on top.

2. HANDLING

  2.1  A parent/guardian may request their own child's records referenced
       in a grievance case; a third-party reporter has no right to
       another student's education records, even the reporter who
       originally filed the complaint.

  2.2  Case details involving a specific student's disciplinary,
       academic, or special-education records are never shown on any
       public-facing dashboard, consistent with the elevated
       confidentiality standard already applied to DHHS mandatory-
       reporting cases under the Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice and DOE Special Education
Services FAQ (records relevant to IEP disputes are covered by this same
FERPA standard).
