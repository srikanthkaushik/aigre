DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
School Transportation Policy
Document No. DOE-POL-012           Effective Date: August 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs regular DOE-operated school bus service [[XREF]]— distinct from DOT
Public Transit Division's city bus service, and distinct from
special-education student transportation (see DOE Special Education
Services FAQ, which is administered under IDEA with its own timelines)[[/XREF]].

2. SCOPE

  2.1  SCHOOL BUS NO-SHOW OR CONSISTENTLY LATE: MEDIUM priority, reviewed
       within 3 business days; a pattern (3+ occurrences) triggers route
       review, similar in spirit to DOT's public-transit service-
       reliability pattern but administered entirely within DOE.

  2.2  BUS SAFETY CONCERN (reckless driving, unsafe stop location):
       Escalated to HIGH priority same day, coordinated with the
       transportation contractor.

  2.3  [[XREF]]This policy does NOT cover general city bus routes used
       incidentally by students — that remains DOT Public Transit
       Division's jurisdiction under DOT Public Transit Service Complaint
       Guide.[[/XREF]]

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOE Special Education Services FAQ (special-needs
transportation) and DOT Public Transit Service Complaint Guide (general
city bus service, not school buses).
