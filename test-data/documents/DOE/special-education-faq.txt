DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Special Education Services FAQ
Last updated: October 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I request a special education evaluation for my child?

A: Submit a written evaluation request through the grievance portal under
"DOE — Special Education Referral," or directly to your child's school.
Under state and federal law (IDEA), DOE has 15 business days to determine
whether to conduct an assessment, and 60 calendar days from parental
consent to complete the evaluation.

Q: My child already has an Individualized Education Program (IEP). Who do
I contact if the school isn't following it?

A: Report through the portal under "DOE — Special Education Referral"
with your child's IEP details. This is treated as a compliance concern
and routed to the DOE Special Education Compliance Office, distinct from
a general school-facilities or bullying complaint, with review initiated
within 10 business days.

Q: Is a delay in receiving special education transportation the same
process as a general DOT transit complaint?

A: No — special-needs student transportation is administered by DOE, not
DOT Public Transit. Report through the "DOE — Special Education Referral"
category even though it involves a vehicle/route.

Q: What if I disagree with the outcome of an evaluation?

A: Parents may request a due-process hearing under IDEA. This is a
formal legal process outside the standard grievance portal SLA structure;
the portal submission triggers a referral to the DOE Due Process Office,
which operates on statutory hearing timelines rather than the citywide
SLA table.
