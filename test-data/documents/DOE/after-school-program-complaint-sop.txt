DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
After-School Program Complaint SOP
Document No. DOE-SOP-014           Effective Date: October 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints about DOE-operated after-school enrichment programs:
supervision quality, program cancellation without notice, and safety
concerns during program hours.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  SAFETY CONCERN DURING PROGRAM HOURS (inadequate supervision leading
       to an incident, unsafe conditions): HIGH priority, reviewed within
       2 business days.

  2.2  PROGRAM CANCELLED WITHOUT ADVANCE NOTICE (leaving working parents
       without childcare on short notice): MEDIUM priority, reviewed
       within 5 business days — primarily a program-management review
       rather than a facility repair, but tracked with a similar
       timeline.

  2.3  This SOP covers DOE-operated programs specifically. Privately-run
       after-school programs that merely rent school space are not DOE's
       responsibility to investigate for program quality, only for
       facility-use compliance.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOE Student Safety & Anti-Bullying Policy (for peer-conflict
issues arising during program hours) and DOE School Facilities
Maintenance SOP.
