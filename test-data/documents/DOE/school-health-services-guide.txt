1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints related to school nurse services, on-campus medication
administration, and general student health accommodations. This is a
DOE-administered process, distinct from DHHS's Food Safety Complaint
Investigation SOP (which covers licensed food-service establishments,
not school health staff) and distinct from a school cafeteria's food
handling, which falls under a separate DOE Nutrition Services
inspection process not detailed in this guide.

2. SCOPE

  2.1  MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION: Complaints that a school nurse or
       designated staff member failed to administer a child's prescribed
       medication as authorized by a parent/guardian medication form.
       Classified HIGH priority given potential health impact; DOE Health
       Services Office reviews within 2 business days and requires the
       school to confirm a corrective process is in place.

  2.2  GENERAL HEALTH ACCOMMODATIONS: Complaints about failure to
       accommodate a documented health condition (e.g., allergy
       protocols, mobility accommodations not health-emergency in
       nature). MEDIUM priority, reviewed within 5 business days.

  2.3  SCHOOL NURSE STAFFING GAPS: Logged for DOE Health Services
       staffing review; no individual-case SLA, as this is a
       resource-planning matter rather than a specific incident.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Food Safety Complaint Investigation SOP (a different
department and a different subject — restaurant/food-establishment
sanitation, not school health staff conduct), and Citywide Escalation &
Safety-Flag Policy for any complaint describing an acute medical
emergency, which should direct the citizen to call 911 rather than rely
on portal SLA timelines.
