DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
Truancy & Attendance Policy
Document No. DOE-POL-017           Effective Date: September 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs how DOE responds to attendance concerns raised through the
portal — most often a parent reporting difficulty getting a child to
attend school regularly, or a concerned third party reporting a student
who appears to be chronically absent.

2. HANDLING

  2.1  PARENT-INITIATED SUPPORT REQUEST (parent struggling to address a
       child's attendance issues, seeking resources): MEDIUM priority,
       connected with a DOE attendance counselor within 5 business days
       — a supportive process, not a punitive one at this stage.

  2.2  THIRD-PARTY REPORT OF SUSPECTED NEGLECT UNDERLYING CHRONIC ABSENCE
       (e.g., a neighbor reporting a child never seems to leave for
       school and describing other concerning signs): Cross-referenced
       against DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide — attendance
       alone is not a mandatory-reporting trigger, but co-occurring
       neglect indicators are.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide.
