DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION
General Citizen Intake FAQ
Last updated: July 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What kinds of issues does DOE handle?

A: School facilities maintenance, student safety and bullying, teacher
conduct, special education services, school health services, school bus
transportation, enrollment boundaries, free/reduced lunch, after-school
programs, ADA accessibility at school buildings, and attendance support.

Q: What does DOE NOT handle?

A: General city bus routes not related to school transportation (DOT),
custody/family-law disputes even when they happen on school property (see
Citywide Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide), and licensed child care for
children under school age (DHHS Child Care Facility Licensing Complaint
SOP, not DOE).

Q: How do I know whether my complaint is about a STUDENT's conduct or a
STAFF member's conduct?

A: This matters — peer-to-peer conduct goes through DOE Student Safety &
Anti-Bullying Policy, while a complaint about a teacher or staff member's
own behavior toward a student goes through DOE Teacher Conduct Complaint
Policy. If you're not sure, describe what happened in detail and intake
will route it correctly.

Q: My concern involves both a facilities issue and a safety issue (like a
broken fence near a street). Does it need to go to two departments?

A: The system dual-tags cases that genuinely span two departments — you
don't need to file two separate reports.
