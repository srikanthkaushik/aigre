DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Food Safety Complaint Investigation SOP
Document No. DHHS-SOP-011          Effective Date: March 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DHHS Environmental Health Division's investigation of citizen
complaints regarding unsanitary conditions, suspected foodborne illness,
or health-code violations at licensed food-service establishments.

2. SCOPE

Applies to restaurants, food trucks, grocery stores, and other licensed
food handlers. Does NOT apply to food distributed by a school cafeteria
(see DOE School Health Services Guide) or complaints about a school
nurse's medication-administration practices, which are education-sector
matters even though both involve health and children.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  SUSPECTED ACTIVE FOODBORNE ILLNESS OUTBREAK (2 or more unrelated
       complaints naming the same establishment within 7 days, each
       describing illness symptoms): Unannounced inspection within 24
       hours, HIGH priority.

  3.2  UNSANITARY CONDITIONS (pest sighting, visible contamination,
       improper food storage temperature reported by a single citizen):
       Unannounced inspection within 5 business days, MEDIUM priority.

  3.3  MINOR / ADMINISTRATIVE (expired permit posting, missing signage):
       Addressed at the establishment's next regularly scheduled
       inspection, LOW priority.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

Consistent with the Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice, the
complainant's identity is not disclosed to the establishment under
investigation unless required by subsequent administrative or legal
proceedings.

5. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Benefits Eligibility & Appeals FAQ (unrelated program area)
and Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy.
