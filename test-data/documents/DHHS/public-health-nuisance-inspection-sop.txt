DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Public Health Nuisance Inspection SOP
Document No. DHHS-SOP-013          Effective Date: May 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DHHS Environmental Health's investigation of public-health
nuisance complaints on PRIVATE property: vector control (rodent/mosquito
breeding conditions), accumulated trash creating a health hazard, and
unsanitary living conditions reported about a residence [[XREF]](distinct from a
licensed food establishment, covered under DHHS Food Safety Complaint
Investigation SOP)[[/XREF]].

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  ACTIVE RODENT INFESTATION reported at a residential property,
       especially where standing water or an open food source is
       described: MEDIUM priority, inspection within 5 business days.

  2.2  STANDING WATER / MOSQUITO BREEDING CONDITIONS: MEDIUM priority,
       inspection within 5 business days; escalated to HIGH during an
       active regional mosquito-borne-illness advisory.

3. DISTINGUISHING FROM OTHER DEPARTMENTS

  3.1  [[XREF]]A pest complaint about a RESTAURANT or licensed food establishment
       goes to DHHS Food Safety Complaint Investigation SOP instead.[[/XREF]]

  3.2  [[XREF]]Illegally dumped material creating standing water on a VACANT LOT
       (not a maintained private residence) may also warrant a DEP
       illegal-dumping report in parallel — the two departments handle
       different aspects (health hazard vs. unauthorized disposal) of the
       same underlying situation.[[/XREF]]

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Food Safety Complaint Investigation SOP and DEP Illegal
Dumping Enforcement Policy.
