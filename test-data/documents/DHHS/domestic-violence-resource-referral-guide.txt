DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Domestic Violence Resource Referral Guide
Document No. DHHS-POL-015          Effective Date: March 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs how the grievance portal handles a submission indicating domestic
violence — routing to confidential resources rather than standard
grievance processing.

2. HANDLING

  2.1  A submission describing an active, in-progress domestic violence
       incident should direct the citizen to call 911 immediately —
       displayed prominently, not buried in standard portal text.

  2.2  A non-emergency request for resources (shelter, legal advocacy,
       restraining order information) is routed to DHHS's confidential
       domestic violence resource line, which operates independently of
       the standard grievance case-tracking system — no case status is
       created in the citizen-visible portal dashboard for these
       requests, per heightened confidentiality need beyond even the
       standard Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice baseline.

  2.3  If the request also involves emergency shelter need, DHHS
       coordinates directly with DHUD's Homelessness Services intake
       out-of-band (not through the standard dual-tag/notification
       mechanism used for ordinary multi-department cases), to avoid
       creating a discoverable case record.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice and DHUD Homelessness
Services & Shelter Referral FAQ (referenced for coordination purposes
only, not as a standard dual-tag scenario).
