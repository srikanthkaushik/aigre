DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Mental Health Crisis Referral Guide
Document No. DHHS-POL-011          Effective Date: October 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs how the grievance portal handles a submission indicating a
mental health crisis — either the citizen's own or a third party's — and
routes it to appropriate resources rather than treating it as a standard
grievance.

2. HANDLING

  2.1  ACTIVE, IMMEDIATE CRISIS (indications of intent to self-harm or
       harm others, in progress): The portal must NOT rely on standard
       SLA timelines. Display crisis-line contact information immediately
       and, if enough detail is provided, flag for CRITICAL-equivalent
       human review per the Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy,
       recognizing this is fundamentally different from a hazard-keyword
       infrastructure case.

  2.2  NON-EMERGENCY REQUEST FOR MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES REFERRAL: Routed
       to DHHS Behavioral Health intake, MEDIUM priority, contact within
       5 business days.

  2.3  CONCERN ABOUT A THIRD PARTY (not the submitter) — for a minor, see
       DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide; for an elderly or
       dependent adult, see DHHS Elder & Adult Protective Services Guide;
       this guide's own scope is primarily first-person requests and
       general crisis-line routing.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy, DHHS Mandatory
Reporting & Escalation Guide, and DHHS Elder & Adult Protective Services
Guide.
