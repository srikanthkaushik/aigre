DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Benefits Appeals Policy
Document No. DHHS-POL-009          Effective Date: January 1, 2022
STATUS: SUPERSEDED — replaced by DHHS-POL-009 Rev. A, effective February 1,
2026. Retained for historical reference only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Establishes the formal appeals process for citizens denied city-
administered emergency assistance benefits.

2. APPEAL WINDOW

  2.1  A citizen must file an appeal within twenty (20) calendar days of
       the denial notice date.

  2.2  Appeals are reviewed by a single Benefits Appeals Officer based on
       the written case file; no expedited review track exists under this
       revision.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

Standard appeals are resolved within forty-five (45) business days of
filing.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Benefits Eligibility & Appeals FAQ (citizen-facing summary,
updated separately when this policy changes).
