DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide
Document No. DHHS-POL-002          Effective Date: July 1, 2022
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

This guide governs the intake and escalation of citizen reports involving
suspected child neglect or abuse, suspected elder/dependent adult abuse
or self-neglect, and other conditions subject to mandatory reporting
requirements under State Welfare & Institutions Code §15630 et seq.

2. INTAKE VIA THE GRIEVANCE PORTAL

  2.1  Reports submitted through the citizen grievance portal describing
       suspected child neglect or abuse are automatically classified
       CRITICAL priority and routed to the DHHS Child Protective Intake
       Unit, bypassing the standard triage queue, per the Citywide
       Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy.

  2.2  The portal is NOT a substitute for calling 911 in the case of an
       immediate, in-progress emergency. Portal guidance text displayed
       to the citizen must make this explicit.

  2.3  Reports are logged with the minimum information necessary to route
       the case (location, nature of concern) and are NOT publicly
       visible in any citizen-facing status dashboard, consistent with
       the Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

CRITICAL-classified mandatory-reporting cases receive an intake-unit
acknowledgment within 1 hour and an in-person or phone follow-up
assessment within 24 hours, consistent with state-mandated response
windows, which supersede the standard citywide SLA table for this case
type.

4. ELDER / DEPENDENT ADULT CONCERNS

See DHHS Elder & Adult Protective Services Guide for the parallel process
covering suspected self-neglect or abuse involving an elderly or
dependent adult, which follows a similar but distinct intake unit and
timeline (HIGH rather than CRITICAL by default, escalating to CRITICAL if
immediate danger is indicated).

5. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy for the hazard-keyword
criteria that trigger automatic CRITICAL classification, and DOE Student
Safety & Anti-Bullying Policy for school-based concerns that may overlap
with mandatory reporting obligations.
