DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Homeless Outreach Coordination Guide
Document No. DHHS-POL-013          Effective Date: December 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Describes DHHS's role in homelessness response: health/social-services
outreach (substance use services, mental health engagement, medical
triage) for individuals experiencing homelessness. This is a distinct but
COORDINATED function alongside DHUD's Homelessness Services & Shelter
Referral process — a citizen report about an encampment or an individual
in crisis may legitimately involve both departments, and the Citywide
Multi-Department Coordination Protocol governs how the two intake
processes stay in sync rather than each running an independent, duplicate
outreach visit.

2. DHHS'S SPECIFIC ROLE

  2.1  DHHS outreach workers focus on health needs: connecting individuals
       to medical care, substance use treatment, and mental health
       services. DHHS does NOT arrange shelter placement directly — that
       is DHUD's function under the Homelessness Services & Shelter
       Referral FAQ.

  2.2  A citizen report primarily describing a HEALTH concern about a
       specific individual (visible injury, apparent medical distress) is
       prioritized to DHHS outreach; a report primarily about SHELTER/
       HOUSING need is prioritized to DHUD, but both departments are
       notified of any encampment-location report regardless of framing.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

Same-day outreach engagement for a report describing an individual in
apparent medical distress; otherwise within 3 business days for a general
encampment/outreach report, coordinated with DHUD's own response window.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Homelessness Services & Shelter Referral FAQ and Citywide
Multi-Department Coordination Protocol.
