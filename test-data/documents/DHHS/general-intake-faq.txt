DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
General Citizen Intake FAQ
Last updated: July 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What kinds of issues does DHHS handle?

A: Food safety at licensed establishments, mandatory reporting (child/
elder welfare concerns), benefits eligibility and appeals, senior
nutrition services, public health nuisances on private property,
immunization clinic access, mental health crisis referrals, homeless
health outreach, and child care facility licensing.

Q: What does DHHS NOT handle?

A: State-administered benefits (SNAP, Medicaid — see Citywide
Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide), K-12 school health services (DOE
School Health Services Guide), and shelter/housing placement for
homelessness (DHUD, though DHHS coordinates on the health side).

Q: A report I filed seems urgent but doesn't fit the standard hazard
categories (gas leak, exposed wiring, etc). What happens?

A: Several DHHS categories — mandatory reporting, active mental health
crisis, unsafe child care conditions — are treated as CRITICAL by their
own subject-matter rules even without matching the citywide hazard
keyword list, because state-mandated timelines or safety implications
apply regardless.

Q: Is my identity kept confidential when I report a concern about someone
else?

A: Yes, consistent with the Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice —
your identity as a reporter is not disclosed to the subject of a
mandatory-reporting or protective-services case.
