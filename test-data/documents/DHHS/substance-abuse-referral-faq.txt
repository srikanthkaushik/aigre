DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Substance Abuse Services Referral FAQ
Last updated: February 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I get a referral to substance abuse treatment for myself?

A: Submit a request through the portal under "DHHS — Behavioral Health
Referral" or call the Behavioral Health line directly. This is a
voluntary, confidential service — no case status is shown on the public-
facing portal dashboard for these requests.

Q: I'm worried about a family member's substance use. Can I report it?

A: You can submit a resource request on their behalf, but DHHS cannot
compel someone into treatment based on a third-party report unless it
rises to the level of an imminent danger (see Mental Health Crisis
Referral Guide) or involves a minor (see Mandatory Reporting & Escalation
Guide).

Q: Is this connected to the homeless outreach program?

A: They can overlap — DHHS Homeless Outreach Coordination Guide covers
substance-use engagement specifically for individuals experiencing
homelessness as part of broader health outreach, while this FAQ covers
the general voluntary-referral pathway available to any resident.
