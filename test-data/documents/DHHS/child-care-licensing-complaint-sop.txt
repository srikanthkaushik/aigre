DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Child Care Facility Licensing Complaint SOP
Document No. DHHS-SOP-017          Effective Date: July 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints about licensed child care facilities (daycares,
in-home care providers) regarding licensing violations: staff-to-child
ratios, facility safety conditions, or unlicensed operation.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  UNSAFE CONDITIONS OR SUSPECTED ABUSE AT A CHILD CARE FACILITY:
       CRITICAL priority, cross-referenced against DHHS Mandatory
       Reporting & Escalation Guide if the report describes suspected
       abuse specifically — same-day licensing inspector dispatch either
       way.

  2.2  RATIO/STAFFING VIOLATION (too many children per caregiver):
       MEDIUM priority, unannounced inspection within 5 business days.

  2.3  SUSPECTED UNLICENSED OPERATION: MEDIUM priority, investigated
       within 10 business days to confirm licensing status before any
       enforcement action.

3. DISTINGUISHING FROM A SCHOOL COMPLAINT

Licensed child care (typically ages 0-5, pre-school) is a DHHS licensing
matter. A complaint about a K-12 public school is DOE's jurisdiction
entirely, even if it involves similar concerns (staff conduct, safety) —
see DOE Teacher Conduct Complaint Policy for the school-side equivalent.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide.
