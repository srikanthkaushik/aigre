DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Immunization Clinic Access FAQ
Last updated: August 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Where can I get a free immunization for my child?

A: DHHS operates walk-in immunization clinics at three community health
sites; no complaint or portal submission is needed to access them — see
the DHHS website for locations and hours. This FAQ addresses portal
complaints ABOUT clinic access, not routine scheduling.

Q: I tried to get my child immunized but was turned away. What do I do?

A: Report through the portal under "DHHS — Immunization Access" with the
clinic location and date. DHHS reviews access-denial reports within 5
business days — this usually indicates a documentation or eligibility
misunderstanding that needs correcting, not an actual policy barrier,
since city immunization clinics do not require proof of insurance or
immigration status.

Q: Is a school vaccination-record dispute a DHHS or DOE matter?

A: If a school is requiring documentation the parent believes is
incorrect or excessive, that is DOE's enrollment/compliance process, not
DHHS — DHHS handles clinic access, not school record requirements.
