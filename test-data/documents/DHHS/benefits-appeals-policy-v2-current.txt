DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Benefits Appeals Policy
Document No. DHHS-POL-009 Rev. A    Effective Date: February 1, 2026
STATUS: CURRENT. Supersedes DHHS-POL-009 (effective January 1, 2022).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Establishes the formal appeals process for citizens denied city-
administered emergency assistance benefits. This revision extends the
appeal window and adds an expedited review track, following the FY2026
Benefits Access Improvement Ordinance.

2. APPEAL WINDOW

  2.1  A citizen must file an appeal within thirty (30) calendar days of
       the denial notice date (extended from 20 days under the prior
       revision).

  2.2  EXPEDITED REVIEW: Appeals citing an emergency circumstance
       (imminent loss of housing, medical necessity) may request
       expedited review at the Appeals Unit's discretion — a track that
       did not exist under the prior revision.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  Standard appeals: resolved within thirty (30) business days of the
       review hearing (improved from 45 business days under the prior
       revision).

  3.2  Expedited appeals: resolved within ten (10) business days.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Benefits Eligibility & Appeals FAQ, which reflects this
current revision's 30-day window and expedited-review option.
