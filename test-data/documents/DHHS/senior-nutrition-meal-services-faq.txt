DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Senior Nutrition & Meal Services FAQ
Last updated: September 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I sign my elderly parent up for home-delivered meals?

A: Apply through the portal under "DHHS — Senior Nutrition" or by phone
to the Senior Services line. Eligibility is based on age (60+) and
inability to prepare meals independently; no income requirement applies.
Enrollment confirmation typically takes 5 business days after application.

Q: A home-delivered meal wasn't delivered on the scheduled day. What do I
do?

A: Report through the portal — this is treated as a MEDIUM priority
service complaint given the population served, with same-day makeup
delivery attempted whenever possible.

Q: Is this the same program as the community senior center lunch?

A: No, congregate dining at senior centers is a separate, walk-in program
with no application required. Home-delivered meals are for individuals
who cannot travel to a center.

Q: My parent seems to be declining and I'm not sure if this is a meal-
program issue or something more serious.

A: If you're concerned about your parent's overall safety or well-being
rather than just meal delivery, that's a matter for DHHS Elder & Adult
Protective Services Guide, a different process with its own intake
channel — mention both concerns if relevant so intake can route
appropriately.
