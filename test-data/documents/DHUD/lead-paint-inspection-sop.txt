DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Lead Paint Hazard Inspection SOP
Document No. DHUD-SOP-013          Effective Date: May 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs inspection of suspected lead-paint hazards in city-subsidized
housing units, particularly units built before 1978 (pre-lead-paint-ban
construction).

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  REPORT INVOLVING A CHILD UNDER 6 WITH SUSPECTED LEAD EXPOSURE
       (peeling/chipping paint, or an elevated blood-lead-level result
       mentioned): HIGH priority per vulnerable-population impact,
       inspection within 24 hours, cross-referenced with DHHS for any
       medical follow-up needed (DHHS handles the health/testing side,
       DHUD the housing-hazard remediation side).

  2.2  GENERAL SUSPECTED LEAD HAZARD (older unit, visible deteriorated
       paint, no specific child-exposure indication): MEDIUM priority,
       inspection within 5 business days.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Public Housing Maintenance SOP (general in-unit hazards)
and DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide (if the report also
raises a broader neglect concern beyond the housing hazard itself).
