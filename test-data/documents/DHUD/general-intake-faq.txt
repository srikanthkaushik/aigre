DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
General Citizen Intake FAQ
Last updated: July 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What kinds of issues does DHUD handle?

A: Habitability complaints in city-subsidized housing, in-unit utility
outages, public housing maintenance, homelessness services, fair housing
discrimination, housing choice vouchers, public housing waitlist,
first-time homebuyer assistance, lead paint hazard inspection, and
accessibility modifications in public housing.

Q: What does DHUD NOT handle?

A: Disputes between private homeowners (out of scope entirely — see
Citywide Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide), general market-rate rental
disputes not involving safety (see the separate Rental Housing Mediation
Program instead), and mortgage refinancing (not covered by the
homebuyer-assistance program).

Q: I'm not sure if my building counts as "city-subsidized." Does that
matter for which process applies?

A: For most complaint types, yes — DHUD's direct code-enforcement
authority is narrower for market-rate housing. But safety hazards (no
heat, structural danger) get a habitability inspection referral
regardless of subsidized status, and fair-housing discrimination
complaints apply to any housing provider in the city. If you're not sure,
describe your unit type in your report and intake will route correctly.
