DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Public Housing Maintenance SOP
Document No. DHUD-SOP-009          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs maintenance of DHUD-owned and operated public housing complexes
(as opposed to privately-owned, city-subsidized rentals — see DHUD Tenant
Complaint & Code Enforcement Guide for that separate category).

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  ELEVATOR OUTAGE: HIGH priority given mobility-impact and
       vulnerable-population considerations. Repair vendor dispatched
       within 4 hours; if repair cannot be completed within 24 hours,
       DHUD must arrange alternate accessibility accommodation (e.g.,
       ground-floor unit transfer) for affected residents with mobility
       needs.

  2.2  MOLD / AIR QUALITY WITHIN A UNIT: MEDIUM priority by default,
       escalated to HIGH if the household includes a child or resident
       with a documented respiratory condition. Inspection within 5
       business days. This is a DHUD in-unit matter — NOT a DEP matter,
       since DEP's Air Quality & Pollution Complaint SOP covers outdoor/
       ambient air, not conditions inside a single housing unit.

  2.3  SIDEWALKS AND GROUNDS on public housing authority property are
       maintained by DHUD directly rather than DPW, as an exception to
       DPW's general sidewalk jurisdiction under Policy 4.2.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Tenant Complaint & Code Enforcement Guide, and DEP Air
Quality & Pollution Complaint SOP (a different scope despite the topical
overlap on "air quality").
