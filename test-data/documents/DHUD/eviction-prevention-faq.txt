DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Eviction Prevention & Rental Assistance FAQ
Last updated: February 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: I received an eviction notice and I'm behind on rent. Can DHUD help?

A: Yes — DHUD's Emergency Rental Assistance program may cover back rent
for eligible households. Apply through the portal under "DHUD — Eviction
Prevention" as soon as possible, since processing takes time relative to
an eviction timeline.

Q: Is this the same as the emergency shelter referral process?

A: No — eviction prevention aims to keep you in your CURRENT home by
covering back rent; emergency shelter referral (see DHUD Homelessness
Services & Shelter Referral FAQ) is for when you have nowhere to stay,
whether or not eviction is the cause. If you're facing both an active
eviction and no backup housing option, mention both in your request so
intake can engage both processes in parallel.

Q: How fast is the review?

A: Given the time-sensitivity of an eviction proceeding, requests citing
an active court date are reviewed within 5 business days; general
requests without an imminent court date are reviewed within 15 business
days.

Q: Does this program require the unit to be city-subsidized?

A: No, this program is available to any income-eligible renter in the
city, not limited to subsidized units — unlike DHUD's general code-
enforcement authority.
