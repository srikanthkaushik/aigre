DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Homelessness Services & Shelter Referral FAQ
Last updated: November 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: I'm about to be evicted and have nowhere to go. Can DHUD help?

A: Yes. Submit a request through the grievance portal under "DHUD —
Emergency Shelter Referral" or call the DHUD Homelessness Response Line
directly (the portal is not the fastest channel for a same-day emergency
— the phone line is staffed 24/7). DHUD Outreach aims to make contact
within 4 hours of an emergency shelter referral request during business
hours, and within 12 hours overnight.

Q: Is there a waitlist for permanent supportive housing?

A: Yes, permanent supportive housing has an eligibility-based waitlist
managed separately from the emergency shelter referral process described
above. Submitting an emergency referral does not automatically place you
on the permanent housing waitlist; that requires a separate application.

Q: I see an encampment on public property and I'm not sure who to
contact.

A: Report the location through the portal under "DHUD — Encampment
Outreach." DHUD Outreach coordinates a response that may include social
services engagement; encampments on private property are handled through
a different process involving the property owner and, if necessary, DPW
for public-right-of-way cleanup.

Q: Does DHUD handle disputes between two private homeowners, like a
property line disagreement?

A: No — that is outside DHUD's jurisdiction entirely. See Citywide
Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide.
