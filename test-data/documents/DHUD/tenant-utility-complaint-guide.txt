Tenant Utility Complaint Guide

STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DHUD's response to a tenant-reported utility outage (heat, hot
water, electricity, water pressure) WITHIN a city-subsidized rental unit,
[[XREF]]as distinct from a city-infrastructure failure affecting an entire street
or neighborhood[[/XREF]].

2. HOW TO TELL THE DIFFERENCE FROM A DPW WATER MAIN ISSUE

  2.1  If ONLY your unit or building is affected, and neighboring
       addresses on the same street report normal service, this is a
       DHUD tenant-utility matter — the landlord is responsible for
       in-building systems (boiler, in-unit plumbing, electrical panel).

  2.2  [[XREF]]If MULTIPLE addresses across a street segment are affected
       simultaneously, this may indicate a city water main issue — see
       DPW Water Main Break Response SOP. DHUD intake staff cross-check
       DPW's active-incident log before dispatching a code-enforcement
       inspector, to avoid duplicate dispatch.[[/XREF]]

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  NO HEAT / NO HOT WATER (single unit or building): HIGH priority,
       inspection within 24 hours, landlord correction order with 48-hour
       compliance deadline (same standard as DHUD Tenant Complaint & Code
       Enforcement Guide Section 3.1 — this guide provides the detailed
       utility-specific procedure referenced there).

  3.2  LOW WATER PRESSURE (not full outage): MEDIUM priority, inspection
       within 5 business days, unless it coincides with a citywide DPW
       advisory in which case it is treated as informational pending DPW
       resolution.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Tenant Complaint & Code Enforcement Guide and DPW Water
Main Break Response SOP.
