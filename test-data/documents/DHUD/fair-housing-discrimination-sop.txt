DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Fair Housing Discrimination Complaint SOP
Document No. DHUD-SOP-011          Effective Date: February 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints alleging housing discrimination based on a protected
category (race, disability, familial status, source of income including
voucher status under city ordinance, and other state/federal protected
classes) by a landlord, property manager, or housing provider.

2. SCOPE

Applies to both subsidized and market-rate housing — unlike DHUD's
general code-enforcement authority (subsidized units only), fair-housing
discrimination jurisdiction extends to any housing provider operating
within city limits.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  A discrimination complaint is reviewed for jurisdiction within 5
       business days, then referred to formal investigation (HIGH
       priority given civil-rights implications) with a target
       determination within 100 calendar days, consistent with typical
       fair-housing investigation timelines — longer than the standard
       citywide SLA table because these are quasi-legal proceedings, not
       infrastructure repairs.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Housing Choice Voucher Program FAQ (source-of-income
discrimination against voucher holders is a common fair-housing
complaint type) and DHUD Tenant Complaint & Code Enforcement Guide
(a separate, narrower process for habitability issues, not
discrimination).
