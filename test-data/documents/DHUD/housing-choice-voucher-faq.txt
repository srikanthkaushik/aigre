DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Housing Choice Voucher Program FAQ
Last updated: June 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I apply for a housing choice voucher (rental assistance)?

A: Applications are accepted during periodic open-enrollment windows
announced by DHUD — check the DHUD website for the current window. This
is a benefits-application process, not a grievance, and is not submitted
through the standard portal complaint flow.

Q: I have a voucher already. Can I use it for any rental unit?

A: Only units meeting program rent and housing-quality standards, with
landlord participation. DHUD Voucher Services can help identify
participating landlords.

Q: My landlord is threatening to stop accepting my voucher. Is that
allowed?

A: This may be a fair-housing issue — see DHUD Fair Housing
Discrimination Complaint SOP, since voucher-holder status is a protected
category under city ordinance in some circumstances. Report through the
portal under "DHUD — Fair Housing" for this specific concern rather than
the general voucher program FAQ.

Q: Is the voucher waitlist the same as the public housing waitlist?

A: No — see DHUD Public Housing Waitlist Eligibility FAQ for the separate
public-housing-unit waitlist, a different program from the voucher
program (which subsidizes rent in privately-owned units).
