DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Accessibility Modification Request Guide
Document No. DHUD-POL-008          Effective Date: July 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs requests from public housing residents for reasonable
accessibility modifications (grab bars, ramps, accessible fixtures) due
to a documented disability, distinct from a routine maintenance repair
under DHUD Public Housing Maintenance SOP.

2. PROCESS

  2.1  Requests require documentation of need (medical provider
       statement) submitted with the request — this is a reasonable-
       accommodation process under fair housing law, not a standard
       maintenance complaint, though it uses the same intake channel.

  2.2  Initial review within 10 business days; installation timeline
       depends on modification complexity, but DHUD must provide an
       interim accommodation plan within that same 10-day window if
       installation will take longer.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Public Housing Maintenance SOP (elevator outages and other
mobility-impact repairs are handled there with their own HIGH-priority
timeline) and DHUD Fair Housing Discrimination Complaint SOP (if a
reasonable-accommodation request is denied without proper justification,
that may itself become a discrimination complaint).
