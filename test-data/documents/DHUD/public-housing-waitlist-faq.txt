DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
Public Housing Waitlist Eligibility FAQ
Last updated: January 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I get on the waitlist for public housing?

A: Apply through the portal under "DHUD — Public Housing Waitlist" or by
paper application. This is a separate waitlist from the Housing Choice
Voucher Program waitlist (see that FAQ) — public housing refers to
DHUD-owned units directly, not privately-owned units with a rent
subsidy.

Q: How long is the waitlist?

A: Wait times vary significantly by unit size and location and are not
governed by a fixed SLA — DHUD publishes estimated wait times by category
on its website, updated quarterly.

Q: Does filing a maintenance complaint at my current public housing unit
affect my position on a waitlist for a different unit (e.g., transfer
request)?

A: No, these are separate processes — see DHUD Public Housing Maintenance
SOP for maintenance complaints about your current unit, which does not
interact with any waitlist status.

Q: I'm currently experiencing homelessness. Does that put me at the top
of the waitlist?

A: Not automatically, but certain emergency preference categories exist.
For an IMMEDIATE emergency shelter need, use DHUD Homelessness Services &
Shelter Referral FAQ instead — the waitlist is for longer-term public
housing placement, not emergency shelter.
