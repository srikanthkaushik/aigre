DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT
First-Time Homebuyer Assistance FAQ
Last updated: March 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Does the city offer any help for first-time homebuyers?

A: Yes — DHUD administers a down-payment assistance program for
qualifying first-time, income-eligible buyers. Apply through the portal
under "DHUD — Homebuyer Assistance."

Q: Can the city help me refinance my existing mortgage?

A: No. This program covers down-payment assistance for a NEW purchase
only — it does not include refinancing assistance for an existing
mortgage. Refinancing is a private lending matter outside DHUD's program
scope; see Citywide Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide for the general
principle that a program's scope doesn't automatically extend to
adjacent, similarly-named needs.

Q: How long does an application take to process?

A: Initial eligibility review takes approximately 20 business days; this
is a benefits-application timeline, not a grievance SLA.

Q: I was denied assistance. Can I appeal?

A: Yes, through the Citywide Grievance Appeals Process, reviewed against
the program's income and first-time-buyer eligibility criteria.
