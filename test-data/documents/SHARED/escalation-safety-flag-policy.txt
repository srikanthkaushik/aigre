CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy
Document No. CITY-POL-001          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT — applies across all departments

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Establishes the citywide criteria under which an incoming grievance is
automatically classified CRITICAL priority and routed ahead of the
standard triage queue, regardless of which department ultimately handles
it. This policy governs classification only; department-specific response
procedures remain in each department's own SOP.

2. HAZARD KEYWORD CRITERIA

A grievance is auto-flagged CRITICAL if its text indicates any of the
following, regardless of department:

  - Active gas leak or gas odor
  - Exposed or downed electrical wiring
  - Structural collapse or imminent collapse risk
  - Child endangerment or suspected abuse/neglect (see DHHS Mandatory
    Reporting & Escalation Guide)
  - Immediate risk of violence or a weapon
  - Dark (unpowered) traffic signal at a high-traffic intersection
  - Active water main break creating a road hazard

3. WHAT DOES NOT TRIGGER AUTO-ESCALATION

Strongly negative sentiment, profanity, or citizen frustration alone does
NOT trigger CRITICAL classification. A repeat submission of an unresolved
MEDIUM or LOW priority issue may justify a one-tier priority bump (see
Citywide SLA Policy Summary), but this is distinct from the hazard-based
CRITICAL classification governed by this policy, and the two should not
be conflated.

4. MULTI-DEPARTMENT CASES

Where a hazard spans more than one department's typical jurisdiction
(e.g., a downed wire over a sidewalk implicates both DOT/DPW right-of-way
and possibly a utility provider), the case is dual-tagged and both
departments are notified; CRITICAL classification applies regardless of
how many departments are tagged.

5. HUMAN REVIEW

Any case where automatic classification confidence is below the
configured threshold is held for human clarification rather than
defaulting to either CRITICAL or a standard tier — see Milestone 4
(agent workflow with human approval gate) for the review-gate mechanism.
