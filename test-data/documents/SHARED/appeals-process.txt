CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Grievance Appeals Process
Document No. CITY-POL-005          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Describes how a citizen may dispute the outcome or classification of a
resolved grievance, [[XREF]]distinct from a benefits-specific appeal (see DHHS
Benefits Eligibility & Appeals FAQ, which is a separate statutory process
with its own timeline)[[/XREF]].

2. WHEN AN APPEAL APPLIES

  2.1  The citizen disagrees with a case being marked RESOLVED when the
       underlying issue persists — this reopens the case per each
       department's reopened-case handling (priority bumped one tier,
       routed back to the original department, flagged for supervisor
       review).

  2.2  The citizen disagrees with the department or priority a case was
       routed to — this may be raised through the portal and is reviewed
       by a supervisor in the originally assigned department, who may
       manually re-route the case [[XREF]](see DPW Resolved Case Log example of
       a case re-classified from missed-collection to illegal dumping)[[/XREF]].

3. TIMELINE

Appeals of a resolved-case classification are reviewed within 10 business
days of the appeal being filed. This is separate from, and does not use,
the priority-tier SLA table in the Citywide SLA Policy Summary, since an
appeal is a review of a closed case rather than a new incident requiring
dispatch.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also [[XREF]]DHHS Benefits Eligibility & Appeals FAQ (a distinct,
statutorily-timed appeals process)[[/XREF]] and Citywide SLA Policy Summary.
