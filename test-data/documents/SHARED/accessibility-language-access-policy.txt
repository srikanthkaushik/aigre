CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Portal Accessibility & Language Access Policy
Document No. CITY-POL-006          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Establishes accessibility (ADA/WCAG) and language-access requirements for
the citizen grievance portal itself, distinct from department-level
physical accessibility policies (e.g., DOE School Facilities
Accessibility Policy, which covers school buildings, not the portal
software).

2. REQUIREMENTS

  2.1  The portal and chatbot interface must meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards
       for screen-reader compatibility, keyboard navigation, and color
       contrast.

  2.2  Complaint submission and status tracking must be available in the
       city's top five most-spoken non-English languages, with
       machine-translation fallback for others, clearly labeled as
       machine-translated.

  2.3  A citizen who cannot use the portal (no internet access, disability
       preventing digital submission) may submit a grievance by phone
       through the same intake pipeline — phone-submitted grievances
       receive identical SLA treatment to portal submissions, not a
       lesser standard.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOE School Facilities Accessibility (ADA) Policy and DHUD
Accessibility Modification Request Guide — both cover physical facility
accessibility, a different scope from this policy's digital/portal
accessibility focus.
