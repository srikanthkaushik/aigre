CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Privacy & PII Handling Notice
Document No. CITY-POL-004          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Describes how personally identifiable information (PII) submitted through
the grievance portal — by citizens describing their own situation or
someone else's — is handled, and establishes the chatbot's obligations
regarding PII.

2. WHAT COUNTS AS PII IN A GRIEVANCE

Social Security numbers, financial account or card numbers, personal
email addresses and phone numbers distinct from the citizen's on-file
contact information, and precise identifying details of a third party
(e.g., a minor named in a mandatory-reporting case) submitted within the
free-text description of a complaint.

3. HANDLING REQUIREMENTS

  3.1  PII embedded in a complaint's free text must be detected and
       redacted before the raw text is stored long-term or forwarded to
       any external LLM provider for classification, sentiment analysis,
       or chatbot response generation.

  3.2  The chatbot must never echo back a citizen's SSN, financial
       account details, or another person's PII, even if directly asked
       to "confirm what's on file." Requests of this nature should be
       refused, with the citizen directed to an authenticated
       account-status channel instead of the chatbot.

  3.3  Case details in sensitive categories (mandatory reporting, elder
       welfare, benefits eligibility) are not shown in any public-facing
       or citizen-status-dashboard view beyond a high-level status label
       (Received / Under Review / Resolved).

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Mandatory Reporting & Escalation Guide and DHHS Benefits
Eligibility & Appeals FAQ, both of which reference this notice for their
confidentiality provisions.
