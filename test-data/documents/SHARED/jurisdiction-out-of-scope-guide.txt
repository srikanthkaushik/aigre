CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide
Document No. CITY-POL-002          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Lists categories of citizen submissions that are outside city jurisdiction
or outside the grievance portal's intended scope, so the system responds
with an accurate "this isn't something we handle" answer rather than
guessing at a department or fabricating a process.

2. OUT OF CITY JURISDICTION (different level of government)

  - Federal matters: tax refunds, Social Security, federal benefits,
    immigration.
  - State matters: state-administered benefits (SNAP, Medicaid, state
    unemployment insurance), state highway maintenance, state court
    matters.
  - These should be acknowledged and, where possible, the citizen pointed
    toward the correct agency, but no city department should be assigned.

3. OUT OF PORTAL SCOPE (city matter, but not a "grievance")

  - Parking ticket appeals: handled through the separate Parking
    Violations Bureau process, not the grievance portal.
  - General praise/compliments with no actionable issue: acknowledged but
    not routed to any department queue.
  - Job applications, procurement/vendor inquiries, public records
    requests: each has its own dedicated city process outside this
    system.

4. PRIVATE CIVIL MATTERS

  - Disputes between two private homeowners (property lines, shared
    fences, private contract disputes) are civil matters between the
    parties, not a city grievance, and are outside DHUD's or any city
    department's jurisdiction to resolve directly.
  - Landlord-tenant disputes in a MARKET-RATE (non-subsidized) unit are
    generally outside DHUD's direct enforcement authority; see DHUD
    Tenant Complaint & Code Enforcement Guide for the narrower set of
    subsidized-housing matters DHUD does handle, and the separate Rental
    Housing Mediation Program for market-rate disputes.

5. WHAT "OUT OF SCOPE" DOES NOT MEAN

A grievance about a topic the corpus doesn't have a specific SOP for is
NOT automatically out of scope — e.g., a full highway repaving project
is a legitimate DOT capital-projects question, just not one with an
individually-tracked point-repair SLA. The system should distinguish "I
don't have this specific information" from "this isn't something the
city handles at all," and say so explicitly rather than guessing.
