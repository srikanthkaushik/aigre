CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Trend Analysis & Recurring Issue Policy
Document No. CITY-POL-008          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Describes how the system identifies recurring/systemic issues across
individually-filed grievances — the basis for the citywide trend
dashboard referenced in the program's business goals (identifying
recurring problems, not just resolving one-off complaints).

2. HOW A TREND IS IDENTIFIED

  2.1  DUPLICATE CLUSTERING: Multiple reports of the same issue at the
       same location (see the duplicate-detection scenario in the
       routing model) feed a location-level recurrence count.

  2.2  CATEGORY-LEVEL VOLUME: A rolling count of grievances per
       category/department/zone is tracked; a statistically significant
       spike relative to the trailing baseline flags the category for
       supervisor review, independent of whether any single case
       breached its SLA.

  2.3  REOPENED-CASE RATE: A department or category with an elevated
       reopened-case rate (see the reopened-complaint scenario) is a
       distinct trend signal from raw volume — it indicates resolutions
       aren't holding, not just that there's a lot of the same complaint.

3. WHAT THIS POLICY DOES NOT COVER

Trend analysis informs supervisor-level review and dashboard reporting;
it does not change any individual case's priority or SLA — a case within
a spiking category is still triaged on its own individual merits.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide SLA Policy Summary (individual-case priority, distinct
from trend-level signals).
