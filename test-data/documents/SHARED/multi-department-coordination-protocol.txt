CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Multi-Department Coordination Protocol
Document No. CITY-POL-007          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Formalizes how two or more departments coordinate on a single grievance
that genuinely spans jurisdiction — dual-tagging, referenced throughout
department policies (e.g., DOE School Facilities Maintenance SOP Section
4's playground-fence example, DHHS Homeless Outreach Coordination Guide's
DHUD coordination, DPW/DEP's protected-tree review sequence).

2. COORDINATION RULES

  2.1  PARALLEL CASES: Both departments' portions run independently with
       their own SLA clocks (e.g., DOE fencing repair and DOT/DPW
       street-side traffic assessment) — one department's delay does not
       pause the other's clock.

  2.2  SEQUENTIAL CASES: One department's action is a precondition for the
       other's (e.g., DPW's hazard assessment before DEP's protected-tree
       removal approval) — the second department's SLA clock starts only
       once the first department's output is available, and this is
       disclosed to the citizen rather than presented as a single
       combined timeline.

  2.3  Every dual-tagged case is visible to both departments' queues;
       neither department can silently drop its portion without a status
       update.

3. RELATED POLICIES

Referenced by: Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy Section 4, DOE
School Facilities Maintenance SOP, DHHS Homeless Outreach Coordination
Guide, DPW Urban Forestry Policy, DEP Tree Preservation Ordinance Policy.
