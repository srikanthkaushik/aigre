1. PURPOSE

Summarizes the citywide priority tiers and their acknowledgment/resolution
windows. This is the same rubric enforced programmatically by the SLA
calculator against the sla_policies reference table — the two are
intentionally kept in sync; if they ever diverge, the sla_policies table
in the systems of record is authoritative for computed due dates, and
this document should be corrected to match.

2. PRIORITY TIERS

  CRITICAL — Ack within 1 hour, resolve within 4 hours.
    Triggered by hazard-keyword criteria (see Citywide Escalation &
    Safety-Flag Policy) or state-mandated timelines (e.g., mandatory
    reporting cases, which follow their own statutory clock but are
    tracked as CRITICAL in the portal).

  HIGH — Ack within 4 hours, resolve within 24 hours.
    Service outages affecting many residents (water main breaks, multi-
    light street outages) or vulnerable-population impact (no-heat
    situations, elder welfare concerns, elevator outages affecting
    mobility-impaired residents).

  MEDIUM — Ack within 1 business day, resolve within 5 business days.
    Individual service degradation, non-urgent code violations, most
    department-level SOP defaults absent a hazard or vulnerable-
    population factor.

  LOW — Ack within 2 business days, resolve within 15 business days.
    Cosmetic or administrative issues, non-urgent scheduling requests.

3. PRIORITY BUMPS

A confirmed repeat submission of the same unresolved issue, combined with
negative sentiment, may justify a one-tier priority bump. Negative
sentiment alone, without a repeat submission, does not change priority
tier — see Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy Section 3.

4. NOTE ON BUSINESS DAYS

"Business days" and "business hours" in this table follow the standard
city government calendar (weekdays, excluding published city holidays).
CRITICAL and HIGH tiers use calendar hours, not business hours, given
their urgency.
