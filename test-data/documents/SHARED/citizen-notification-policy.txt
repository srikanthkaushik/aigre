CITYWIDE GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION ENGINE
Citizen Notification & Communication Policy
Document No. CITY-POL-009          Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs when and how citizens are notified of status changes to their
grievance, and how notification preferences (email vs. phone/SMS) are
honored.

2. NOTIFICATION TRIGGERS

  2.1  Citizens are notified at: initial triage/classification, any
       status change (ROUTED, IN_PROGRESS, ESCALATED, RESOLVED,
       REOPENED), and if the case is auto-linked as a DUPLICATE (with a
       plain-language explanation that it's linked to an already-open
       case, not simply dismissed).

  2.2  A grievance with no citizen contact information on file (an
       anonymous or phone-line-captured submission with no usable
       contact method) cannot receive proactive notifications — the
       citizen must check status manually via the portal using their
       case reference number.

  2.3  Notification preference honors the citizen's `preferred_contact`
       setting (email or phone); if neither is on file, no proactive
       notification is attempted.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Privacy & PII Handling Notice (what CAN be included in
a notification — high-level status only, no sensitive case detail for
DHHS-category cases).
