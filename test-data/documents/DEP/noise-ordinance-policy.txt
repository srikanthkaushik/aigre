DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Noise Ordinance Enforcement Policy
Document No. DEP-POL-012           Effective Date: July 1, 2023
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DEP's enforcement of City Code §21-1 ("Noise Control Ordinance"),
covering excessive or prohibited noise from residential, commercial, and
construction sources.

2. DEP JURISDICTION VS. POLICE JURISDICTION

  2.1  DEP handles noise complaints involving a MEASURABLE or ONGOING
       source: barking dogs, HVAC/mechanical equipment, construction
       outside permitted hours, commercial music/amplified sound. DEP
       may dispatch a noise-monitoring technician to take a decibel
       reading against the ordinance threshold.

  2.2  Noise involving an ACTIVE DISTURBANCE requiring immediate
       in-person response (a loud party happening right now, a
       physical altercation) is a police matter, not a DEP matter — the
       grievance portal should direct citizens to the non-emergency
       police line for in-progress disturbances rather than log it as a
       standard DEP case.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  RECURRING SOURCE (reported 2+ times, same source, e.g. a barking
       dog most nights): MEDIUM priority, monitoring visit scheduled
       within 5 business days.

  3.2  CONSTRUCTION NOISE OUTSIDE PERMITTED HOURS: MEDIUM priority,
       investigated within 3 business days; repeat violations may result
       in permit suspension.

4. NOT A DEP MATTER

A report framed around concern for a person's welfare (e.g., "I'm worried
about my elderly neighbor") is not a noise complaint even if noise is
mentioned — route to DHHS Elder & Adult Protective Services Guide if the
substance of the concern is the person's well-being rather than the noise
itself.

5. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHHS Elder & Adult Protective Services Guide (different concern,
same neighbor-reported channel) and Citywide Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope
Guide for the police-matter distinction in Section 2.2.
