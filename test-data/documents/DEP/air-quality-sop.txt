DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Air Quality & Pollution Complaint SOP
Document No. DEP-SOP-015           Effective Date: February 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DEP's investigation of citizen reports concerning outdoor/ambient
air quality: industrial smoke, chemical odors, visible emissions from a
facility, or open burning.

2. SCOPE

Applies to OUTDOOR, ambient-air conditions traceable to an identifiable
or suspected source (a facility, vehicle, or open burning). Does NOT
apply to indoor air quality within a single housing unit — see DHUD
Public Housing Maintenance SOP for mold/air-quality complaints inside a
public housing unit, which is an entirely different jurisdiction despite
the shared topic of "air quality."

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  ACTIVE VISIBLE EMISSION OR STRONG CHEMICAL ODOR from an
       identifiable facility: HIGH priority, field technician dispatched
       within 4 hours to assess and, if warranted, take air-quality
       readings.

  3.2  INTERMITTENT OR MILD ODOR COMPLAINT: MEDIUM priority, logged and
       investigated within 5 business days; multiple reports from the
       same area within a short window may trigger an expedited
       facility inspection.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Public Housing Maintenance SOP (in-unit air quality, a
different scope) and Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy for hazard
criteria that could indicate a chemical release requiring immediate
CRITICAL classification.
