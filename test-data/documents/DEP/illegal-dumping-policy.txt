DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Illegal Dumping Enforcement Policy
Document No. DEP-POL-007           Effective Date: April 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DEP's response to citizen reports of illegally dumped material —
debris, furniture, appliances, or hazardous waste disposed of on public
or private property without authorization, in violation of City Code
§19-3.

2. DISTINGUISHING FROM A MISSED TRASH COLLECTION

  2.1  [[XREF]]If the citizen's own household material was properly set out on a
       scheduled collection day and simply not picked up, that is a DPW
       matter under DPW Trash & Recycling Collection SOP — NOT illegal
       dumping.[[/XREF]]

  2.2  Illegal dumping involves material deposited by an unknown or
       unauthorized third party, in a location not designated for
       disposal (vacant lots, alleys, roadsides, someone else's
       property), often without the property owner's knowledge.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  HAZARDOUS MATERIAL (chemicals, medical waste, unidentified
       containers): CRITICAL priority, DEP Hazmat Response dispatched
       same day per the Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy.

  3.2  BULK NON-HAZARDOUS DEBRIS (furniture, construction material,
       household junk): MEDIUM priority, site assessment within 5
       business days, removal scheduled based on volume.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also [[XREF]]DPW Trash & Recycling Collection SOP (the department to route
to if the report is actually about a missed pickup)[[/XREF]] and Citywide
Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy.
