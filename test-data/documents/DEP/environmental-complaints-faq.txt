DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Citizen FAQ: Environmental Complaints
Last updated: January 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What's the difference between illegal dumping and a missed trash
pickup?

A: If it's your own household trash that you set out for scheduled
collection and it wasn't picked up, that's a DPW matter (see DPW Trash &
Recycling Collection SOP). If someone else dumped material somewhere it
doesn't belong — a vacant lot, alley, or your property without your
knowledge — that's illegal dumping, a DEP matter.

Q: Is a barking dog a police matter or a DEP matter?

A: An ongoing/recurring barking-dog complaint is handled by DEP as a
noise-ordinance matter, with a monitoring visit scheduled within days,
not immediately. If the situation is an active, in-progress disturbance
requiring someone to respond right now, contact the non-emergency police
line instead.

Q: My apartment has mold. Is that a DEP air-quality issue?

A: No — indoor air quality and mold within a single housing unit is
handled by DHUD (public/subsidized housing) as a habitability matter, not
DEP. DEP handles outdoor/ambient air quality from an external source like
a factory or open burning.

Q: How do I report someone burning trash in their backyard?

A: Report through the portal under "DEP — Air Quality." Open burning
within city limits is generally prohibited outside of permitted
exceptions; DEP dispatches based on the active-emission response
timeline if the burning is ongoing at time of report.
