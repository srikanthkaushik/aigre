DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Vehicle Emissions & Idling Complaint SOP
Document No. DEP-SOP-023           Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints about excessive vehicle idling (in violation of the
citywide 5-minute idling ordinance) and visible excessive vehicle
emissions from commercial fleets (delivery trucks, buses) operating
within city limits.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  RECURRING IDLING AT A SPECIFIC LOCATION (e.g., delivery trucks
       idling outside a residence regularly): MEDIUM priority,
       monitoring/outreach to the responsible company within 5 business
       days.

  2.2  VISIBLE EXCESSIVE EMISSIONS FROM A SPECIFIC VEHICLE (heavy black
       smoke): MEDIUM priority if a one-time report, escalated if
       multiple citizens report the same vehicle/route.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DEP Air Quality & Pollution Complaint SOP (facility-based
emissions, a different source category from individual vehicles) and
DEP Noise Ordinance Enforcement Policy (idling complaints sometimes
overlap with a noise complaint about the same vehicle).
