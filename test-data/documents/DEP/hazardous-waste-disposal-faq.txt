DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Hazardous Waste Disposal FAQ
Last updated: November 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I dispose of household hazardous waste (paint, batteries,
chemicals)?

A: The city holds periodic household hazardous waste collection events —
check the DEP website for the next date and location. This is a
scheduled drop-off program, not something you report through the
grievance portal.

Q: I found an abandoned container of unknown chemicals in a vacant lot.
What do I do?

A: Do not approach it. Report through the portal under "DEP — Hazardous
Materials" or call DEP's hazmat line directly. This is treated as
CRITICAL priority per the Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy
(hazardous material) and DEP Hazmat Response is dispatched same day.

Q: Is this the same process as reporting illegal dumping?

A: Related but distinct — see DEP Illegal Dumping Enforcement Policy for
general non-hazardous dumped material (furniture, construction debris).
Anything that could be a chemical, medical, or otherwise hazardous
substance escalates through this hazmat process instead, regardless of
whether it was "dumped" or simply abandoned.
