Water Quality Testing Complaint SOP
Document No. DEP-SOP-019           Effective Date: June 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DEP's investigation of citizen-reported drinking water QUALITY
concerns — taste, odor, color, or suspected contamination — distinct from
a DPW Water Main Break, which concerns supply/pressure/infrastructure
failure, not water quality itself. A citizen can have normal pressure and
still have a legitimate quality complaint, or vice versa; the two SOPs
address genuinely different problems even though both involve "the
water."

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  SUSPECTED CONTAMINATION (unusual color, chemical odor, multiple
       reports from the same area): HIGH priority, DEP water quality
       technician dispatched within 4 hours to sample and test.

  2.2  TASTE/ODOR COMPLAINT WITHOUT CONTAMINATION INDICATORS (single
       report, "tastes a bit off"): MEDIUM priority, sample kit provided
       and results returned within 5 business days — often attributable
       to routine seasonal mineral variation or aging in-home plumbing,
       not a system-wide issue.

  2.3  If DEP's testing indicates the issue originates at the water main
       level (pressure/infrastructure) rather than water quality, the
       case is referred to DPW under Water Main Break Response SOP.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DPW Water Main Break Response SOP (infrastructure/supply, not
quality) and DHUD Tenant Utility Complaint Guide (in-unit utility outage,
also a different concern from water quality).
