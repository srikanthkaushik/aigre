DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Recycling & Composting Program FAQ
Last updated: October 2025

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I get a larger recycling bin, or sign up for the composting
program?

A: Submit a request through the portal under "DEP — Recycling Program."
This covers PROGRAM PARTICIPATION (bin size, enrollment, what materials
are accepted) [[XREF]]— a different concern from a missed pickup, which is DPW's
Trash & Recycling Collection SOP[[/XREF]].

Q: My recycling wasn't picked up this week. Is that a DEP or DPW issue?

A: [[XREF]]DPW — routine collection service (including missed pickups) is
handled under DPW Trash & Recycling Collection SOP.[[/XREF]] DEP's role here is
limited to the program design (what's recyclable, bin sizing, composting
enrollment), not day-to-day collection operations.

Q: What materials are accepted in curbside composting?

A: See the current accepted-materials list on the DEP website; this
changes periodically as the program expands and isn't tracked as a
grievance-portal item.

Q: I think my neighbor is putting the wrong materials in the shared
recycling and it's causing contamination issues. Is that reportable?

A: You can report it as a program-compliance concern through the portal
under "DEP — Recycling Program," reviewed within 10 business days;
this is an educational/compliance matter, not an emergency, and DEP
typically starts with outreach rather than enforcement.
