DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Construction Dust & Erosion Control SOP
Document No. DEP-SOP-025           Effective Date: March 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints about excessive dust or uncontrolled erosion from an
active construction site affecting surrounding properties or public
right-of-way.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  RECURRING/MULTI-HOUSEHOLD DUST COMPLAINT (visible dust covering
       cars, surfaces, over multiple days, from the same site): MEDIUM
       priority, site inspection within 5 business days to verify
       compliance with the site's dust-control permit conditions.

  2.2  EROSION RUNOFF ENTERING A STORM DRAIN OR WATERWAY: Escalated and
       cross-referenced with DEP Wetlands & Stormwater Runoff Policy —
       treated as HIGH priority given contamination risk, not just a
       nuisance-level dust complaint.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DEP Wetlands & Stormwater Runoff Policy and DEP Noise Ordinance
Enforcement Policy (construction sites often generate both types of
complaint simultaneously, handled as related but separately-tracked
concerns).
