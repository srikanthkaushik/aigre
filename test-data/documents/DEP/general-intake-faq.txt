DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
General Citizen Intake FAQ
Last updated: July 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What kinds of issues does DEP handle?

A: Illegal dumping, noise ordinance enforcement, air quality/pollution,
drinking water quality testing, recycling/composting program design,
pesticide/herbicide complaints, wetlands and stormwater contamination,
hazardous waste/hazmat response, tree preservation (protected trees), and
vehicle emissions/idling complaints.

Q: What does DEP NOT handle?

A: Routine trash/recycling pickup (DPW), water main breaks and pressure
issues (DPW), routine right-of-way tree maintenance for non-protected
trees (DPW Urban Forestry, though DEP handles protected-tree status
determinations), and storm drain mechanical blockages without
contamination (DPW).

Q: A lot of DEP topics sound similar to DPW topics (water, trees, drains).
How does the system tell them apart?

A: By the specific nature of the concern — DEP generally covers quality/
contamination/regulatory-compliance angles, while DPW covers
infrastructure operation and maintenance. If you're not sure, describe
what you're actually observing (a taste/smell/visible contamination vs.
a broken pipe or missed pickup) and intake will route correctly.
