DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Wetlands & Stormwater Runoff Policy
Document No. DEP-POL-017           Effective Date: August 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DEP's protection of designated wetlands and enforcement of
stormwater runoff regulations — construction-site erosion escaping into
a waterway, illegal filling of a wetland, or contaminated discharge from
a storm drain outfall.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  ACTIVE CONTAMINATED DISCHARGE (visible discoloration, sheen, or
       odor at a storm drain outfall into a waterway): HIGH priority,
       DEP dispatched within 4 hours to sample and trace the source.

  2.2  SUSPECTED UNAUTHORIZED WETLAND FILLING OR CONSTRUCTION ENCROACHMENT:
       MEDIUM priority, site investigation within 10 business days given
       the need to verify wetland boundaries against survey records.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also [[XREF]]DPW Storm Drain & Catch Basin Maintenance SOP (mechanical
blockage/flooding, a different concern from contamination)[[/XREF]] and DEP
Construction Dust & Erosion Control SOP.
