DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION
Pesticide & Herbicide Application Complaint SOP
Document No. DEP-SOP-021           Effective Date: April 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs complaints about pesticide or herbicide application on city
property (parks, right-of-way landscaping) or complaints about a
licensed commercial applicator's practices affecting a neighboring
property (drift, improper notification).

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  REPORTED EXPOSURE SYMPTOM (skin/respiratory reaction reported at
       time of or shortly after application): HIGH priority, DEP
       investigates within 4 hours, cross-referenced with DHHS if medical
       attention was sought.

  2.2  NOTIFICATION/DRIFT COMPLAINT WITHOUT REPORTED SYMPTOMS (city
       failed to post required notification signage, or drift onto a
       neighboring yard): MEDIUM priority, investigated within 5 business
       days.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DEP Tree Preservation Ordinance Policy (both may be relevant
where herbicide use affects protected trees) and DHHS Public Health
Nuisance Inspection SOP (a different scope — pest/vector conditions, not
chemical application complaints).
