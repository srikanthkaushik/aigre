CITY DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Traffic Signal Repair & Timing Policy
Document No. DOT-POL-009           Effective Date: June 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

This policy governs DOT's response to citizen-reported traffic signal
malfunctions, including signals stuck on a single color, dark (unpowered)
signals, and mistimed pedestrian crossing signals. It also covers
staffing of school-zone crossing guard posts, which DOT co-administers
with the school district under Interagency Agreement IA-2021-07.

2. CLASSIFICATION AND RESPONSE

  2.1  DARK SIGNAL (no power / completely unlit): Treated as an
       intersection-control failure. Dispatch within 1 hour. If DOT crews
       cannot restore power within 2 hours, temporary stop-sign control
       and/or a traffic officer will be posted per the Citywide
       Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy.

  2.2  STUCK SIGNAL (fixed on red, green, or flashing improperly):
       Dispatch within 4 hours during business hours; next business day
       if reported after 6:00 PM.

  2.3  MISTIMED PEDESTRIAN SIGNAL (walk cycle too short, doesn't trigger
       on button press): Logged for the next scheduled signal-timing
       review, target resolution within 15 business days, unless the
       location is adjacent to a school (see 2.4).

  2.4  SCHOOL-ZONE SIGNALS AND CROSSING GUARDS: Malfunctions or staffing
       gaps at signals within 500 feet of a K-8 school are prioritized to
       the same-day response window whenever reported before 2:00 PM on a
       school day. Crossing-guard staffing gaps are logged and referred
       to the DOT School Safety Liaison; DOT does not employ crossing
       guards directly — staffing is coordinated with DOE (see DOE
       Student Safety & Anti-Bullying Policy for related school-zone
       safety provisions) but DOT retains responsibility for the physical
       signal equipment.

3. REPORTING CHANNELS

Citizens may report signal issues through the grievance portal, the DOT
24-hour signal hotline, or via referral from police dispatch for
signal-related incidents.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOT Road Maintenance SOP v2 for road-surface defects near
signalized intersections, and DOT Street Signage & Visibility Policy for
non-signal traffic control devices (stop signs, yield signs, crosswalk
markings).
