CITY DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Railroad Crossing Safety Policy
Document No. DOT-POL-023           Effective Date: January 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DOT's coordination role for citizen-reported hazards at
at-grade railroad crossings within city limits. The crossing gates,
signals, and track itself are owned and maintained by the railroad
operator, not the city — DOT's role is limited to the adjacent roadway
approach and coordinating hazard reports with the railroad's dispatch.

2. RESPONSE STANDARDS

  2.1  MALFUNCTIONING CROSSING GATE (stuck up, stuck down, no warning
       lights): DOT immediately relays the report to the railroad
       operator's emergency dispatch line (a CRITICAL-equivalent hazard
       under the Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy) and posts a
       temporary traffic officer at the crossing if the gate is stuck up
       (unsafe) while awaiting the railroad's response — DOT does not
       repair the gate itself.

  2.2  ROADWAY APPROACH DEFECT (pothole, faded markings on the city side
       of the crossing): Handled under standard DOT Road Maintenance SOP
       v2 / Pavement Marking Program, as this portion is city-maintained.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide (the crossing
equipment itself is not a city asset) and Citywide Escalation &
Safety-Flag Policy.
