CITY DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Pavement Marking Program
Document No. DOT-POL-020           Effective Date: March 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Describes the citywide pavement-marking survey and repainting program
covering crosswalks, lane lines, turn arrows, and parking-space striping.
Expands on the brief reference in DOT Street Signage & Visibility Policy
Section 2.3.

2. PROGRAM STRUCTURE

  2.1  Markings are surveyed for wear on an 18-month rotating cycle by
       zone, not on-demand per citizen report.

  2.2  A citizen report of a specific faded marking is logged against that
       zone's survey record and may accelerate the zone's position in the
       queue if multiple independent reports accumulate for the same
       location, but there is still NO individually tracked SLA or
       guaranteed completion date communicated for a single report — the
       correct response to a citizen asking "when will this be fixed" is
       that it's part of the ongoing survey cycle, not a specific date.

  2.3  EXCEPTION: a school-zone crosswalk with markings worn to the point
       of being effectively invisible is escalated for out-of-cycle
       repainting within 30 business days, given the safety implication.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOT Street Signage & Visibility Policy and DOT School Zone
Safety Program.
