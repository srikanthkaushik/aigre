DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
General Citizen Intake FAQ
Last updated: July 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What kinds of issues does DOT handle?

A: Road surface defects (potholes, pavement), traffic signals, street
signage and pavement markings, public transit service, bike lanes, school
zone traffic safety, and the roadway approach to railroad crossings. See
the individual DOT policies for specific SLAs.

Q: What does DOT NOT handle?

A: Sidewalks (DPW), streetlights (DPW), parking ticket appeals (separate
Parking Violations Bureau process), full road repaving/capital projects
(DOT Capital Projects schedule, not the point-repair SOPs), and rideshare/
taxi service complaints (mostly out of city jurisdiction — see DOT
Vehicle-for-Hire Complaint Guide).

Q: How do I know if my issue is urgent enough to be marked CRITICAL?

A: You don't need to guess — the system automatically flags hazard
conditions (dark signals at busy intersections, exposed wiring, active
gas leaks) as CRITICAL per the Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy
regardless of how you word your report.

Q: Can I submit the same issue more than once if I don't hear back?

A: You can, but duplicate reports of the same issue at the same location
are automatically linked rather than opening a second, separate case with
its own timeline — the original report's timeline still applies.
