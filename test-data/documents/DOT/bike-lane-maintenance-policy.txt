CITY DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Bike Lane Maintenance Policy
Document No. DOT-POL-016           Effective Date: May 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DOT's response to citizen-reported hazards in dedicated bicycle
lanes: debris, faded lane markings, damaged protective bollards, and
blocked lanes (illegally parked vehicles).

2. RESPONSE STANDARDS

  2.1  DEBRIS OR GLASS IN BIKE LANE: Same-day sweep dispatch if reported
       before 2:00 PM, next business day otherwise.

  2.2  DAMAGED PROTECTIVE BOLLARD (knocked down, missing): Treated similarly
       to a knocked-down stop sign — temporary cone/barrier within 4 hours,
       permanent replacement within 5 business days.

  2.3  BLOCKED LANE (illegally parked vehicle): Not a DOT maintenance
       matter — refer citizen to non-emergency police/parking enforcement
       for active vehicle removal.

  2.4  FADED LANE MARKINGS: Logged for the citywide repainting survey
       cycle, same as crosswalk markings under DOT Street Signage &
       Visibility Policy — no individually tracked SLA for an isolated
       report.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOT Street Signage & Visibility Policy and DOT Pavement Marking
Program.
