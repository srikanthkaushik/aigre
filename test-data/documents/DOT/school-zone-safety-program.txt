CITY DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
School Zone Safety Program
Document No. DOT-POL-018           Effective Date: August 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Describes DOT's school-zone-specific traffic infrastructure program:
reduced speed limit signage, flashing beacon activation windows, and the
annual school-zone safety audit conducted jointly with DOE.

2. PROGRAM ELEMENTS

  2.1  FLASHING BEACON MALFUNCTION: A school-zone flashing beacon that
       fails to activate during posted school hours is treated under DOT
       Traffic Signal Repair & Timing Policy's dark-signal protocol
       (same-day response), since it is a safety-critical failure during
       active dismissal/arrival windows.

  2.2  ANNUAL AUDIT: Each school zone is audited jointly with DOE once per
       year for sightline obstructions, signage condition, and crossing
       guard post adequacy. Citizen reports outside the audit cycle are
       still individually triaged, not deferred to the next audit.

  2.3  NEW SCHOOL-ZONE DESIGNATION REQUESTS: A request to designate a new
       street segment as a school zone (e.g., after a school relocation)
       is a planning request, not a complaint, and is reviewed on DOT's
       quarterly planning calendar rather than the standard SLA table.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOT Traffic Signal Repair & Timing Policy Section 2.4 and DOE
School Facilities Maintenance SOP Section 4 for the coordination protocol
on street-adjacent hazards.
