CITY DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Street Signage & Visibility Policy
Document No. DOT-POL-011           Effective Date: September 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DOT's response to citizen reports concerning non-signal traffic
control devices: stop signs, yield signs, speed limit signs, and
crosswalk pavement markings. This policy does not cover electronic
traffic signals (see DOT Traffic Signal Repair & Timing Policy) or
directional/wayfinding signage on state highways (not city jurisdiction —
see Citywide Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide).

2. RESPONSE STANDARDS

  2.1  KNOCKED-DOWN OR MISSING STOP/YIELD SIGN: Treated as a critical
       intersection-control failure. DOT dispatches a temporary sign or
       barricade within 4 hours of report, with permanent replacement
       within 5 business days.

  2.2  OBSCURED SIGN (overgrown foliage, graffiti, faded reflectivity):
       Logged for the next scheduled maintenance sweep of that zone,
       target resolution within 20 business days. Reports involving a
       school zone are prioritized to 10 business days.

  2.3  CROSSWALK PAVEMENT MARKINGS (faded lines, missing markings): DOT
       maintains a citywide repainting schedule based on wear surveys
       conducted every 18 months. Individual citizen reports of faded
       crosswalk markings are logged to inform the next survey cycle but
       do NOT have an individually tracked SLA under this policy — see
       Citywide Jurisdiction & Out-of-Scope Guide for how such requests
       are communicated to citizens.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOT Road Maintenance SOP v2 for pavement-surface defects
(distinct from painted markings), and the Citywide Escalation &
Safety-Flag Policy for hazard criteria.
