CITY DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
Winter Road Treatment SOP
Document No. DOT-SOP-022           Effective Date: October 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DOT's PROACTIVE road treatment program (salt brine pre-treatment
ahead of forecast winter weather) — distinct from DPW's REACTIVE snow
plowing/removal during and after a storm, covered separately under DPW
Snow Removal Priority Routes Policy. DOT treats the road surface itself
(chemical de-icing) before and during a storm; DPW physically clears
accumulated snow. Both departments may be active on the same street during
a single storm event, and citizen reports are routed based on the specific
complaint (ice/slickness vs. unplowed snow accumulation).

2. TREATMENT PRIORITY

  2.1  Pre-treatment follows the same Tier 1/2/3 route priority as DPW's
       snow removal tiers (Tier 1 = hospital/fire/bus routes, etc.), applied
       12-24 hours ahead of a forecast winter storm where feasible.

  2.2  BLACK ICE REPORT (no visible snow, but a citizen reports a
       slippery/icy road surface): Treated as CRITICAL if on a Tier 1 or
       Tier 2 route, HIGH if Tier 3 — dispatched for emergency spot
       treatment regardless of the standing pre-treatment schedule.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DPW Snow Removal Priority Routes Policy (physical plowing, a
separate but coordinated process) and Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag
Policy.
