DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION — PUBLIC TRANSIT DIVISION
Citizen FAQ: Bus and Transit Service Complaints
Last updated: February 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: My bus route consistently skips my stop. What do I do?

A: Report the route number, stop location, and approximate time(s) of the
missed stop(s) through the grievance portal under "Public Transit." DOT
Transit Operations reviews GPS/AVL (automatic vehicle location) records
against the reported time and, where a pattern of 3 or more missed stops
in a 30-day window is confirmed, opens a formal service-reliability
review of the route. A single reported incident is logged but does not by
itself trigger an operator investigation.

Q: A bus was significantly late or never arrived. Can I get a refund?

A: Fare disputes are handled by Transit Customer Service directly, not
through the grievance portal. If your complaint is about a
service-reliability pattern rather than a single fare dispute, use the
portal as described above.

Q: Is a broken bus shelter or missing bench a DOT or DPW issue?

A: Bus shelters, benches, and posted schedules at transit stops are DOT
Public Transit Division assets. Report through the portal under "Public
Transit — Stop Infrastructure." The surrounding sidewalk itself remains a
DPW responsibility (see DPW Sidewalk Maintenance Policy).

Q: How do I report an unsafe or rude bus operator?

A: Operator conduct complaints go to Transit Customer Service, not the
grievance portal, because they involve a personnel process rather than an
infrastructure or service defect. If the complaint involves a safety
incident (collision, injury), it should be reported immediately by phone
to the DOT 24-hour safety line in addition to any other channel.

Q: What's the difference between a "service reliability" complaint and a
"route planning" request?

A: Service reliability concerns whether the currently scheduled service is
actually running as scheduled. Requests to add, remove, or reroute a bus
line are route-planning requests, handled through the DOT Transit Planning
public comment process (a separate, quarterly process), not the grievance
portal.
