CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Graffiti Removal Policy
Document No. DPW-POL-019           Effective Date: June 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs removal of graffiti from DPW-owned infrastructure: bridges,
retaining walls, public buildings, and street furniture. Expands on the
brief mention in DPW Citizen FAQ — Public Works Services.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  GRAFFITI CONTAINING HATE SPEECH OR THREATS: Treated as HIGH
       priority regardless of surface owner, removal within 24 hours
       given community-impact sensitivity, coordinated with police if the
       content suggests a targeted threat.

  2.2  STANDARD GRAFFITI on DPW-owned surfaces: MEDIUM priority, removal
       within 15 business days.

  2.3  GRAFFITI ON PRIVATE PROPERTY: Not a DPW removal responsibility —
       the property owner is responsible, though DPW's voluntary
       removal-assistance program (separate application process, not this
       SLA) can help eligible property owners.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy (for the hate-speech/
threat escalation) and DPW Citizen FAQ — Public Works Services.
