DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Citizen FAQ: Public Works Services
Last updated: January 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: A city tree near my house dropped a large branch during a storm and
damaged my fence. Who handles this?

A: DPW Urban Forestry manages trees in the public right-of-way (between
the sidewalk and the street, or in a designated tree lawn). Report storm
damage through the portal under "Public Works — Trees." Property damage
claims are handled through the separate Risk Management claims process,
not the grievance portal directly, though the initial report starts that
process.

Q: How do I schedule a bulk-item pickup (furniture, appliances)?

A: Bulk-item pickup is scheduled separately from routine weekly
collection — request a pickup date through the portal at least 5 business
days in advance. This is a scheduling request, not a complaint, and has
no SLA under DPW-SOP-030.

Q: Is graffiti removal a DPW responsibility?

A: Graffiti on DPW-owned infrastructure (bridges, retaining walls, public
buildings) is removed by DPW within 15 business days of report. Graffiti
on private property is the owner's responsibility, though DPW maintains a
voluntary removal-assistance program citizens can apply for separately.

Q: Who do I contact about a dead animal on a public street?

A: Report through the portal under "Public Works — Animal Removal." DPW
targets same-day or next-business-day removal depending on report time.

Q: What's the difference between a "Public Works" complaint and reporting
illegal dumping?

A: If material was properly set out for scheduled collection and simply
missed, that's a DPW trash-collection issue (see DPW Trash & Recycling
Collection SOP). If someone dumped material illegally in a location not
intended for disposal, that's a DEP Illegal Dumping matter.
