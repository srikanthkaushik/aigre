CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Street Lighting Maintenance Policy
Policy 6.1                          Effective Date: November 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DPW's response to citizen reports of malfunctioning, dark, or
damaged streetlights on public right-of-way. Does not cover traffic
signal lighting (see DOT Traffic Signal Repair & Timing Policy) or
lighting on private property.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  SINGLE DARK STREETLIGHT: Logged and scheduled for the next
       maintenance route pass, target repair within 10 business days.

  2.2  MULTIPLE ADJACENT DARK STREETLIGHTS (3 or more on the same block,
       suggesting a circuit-level fault): Escalated to Electrical
       Operations for same-week investigation, target repair within 5
       business days, because a multi-light outage indicates a shared
       infrastructure fault rather than a single bulb failure.

  2.3  DAMAGED POLE (leaning, exposed wiring, vehicle-strike damage):
       Treated as a hazard under the Citywide Escalation & Safety-Flag
       Policy. Emergency crew dispatched same day to secure the site;
       full repair timeline assessed on-site.

3. REPORTING NOTE

A report of "it's dark on my street at night" without specifying a
malfunctioning fixture is logged as a lighting-adequacy request (a
separate, non-SLA planning process) rather than a maintenance repair
under this policy.

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOT Traffic Signal Repair & Timing Policy, and Citywide
Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy.
