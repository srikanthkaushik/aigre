CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Sidewalk Maintenance Policy
Policy 4.2                          Effective Date: April 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Pursuant to City Code §16-5 ("Pedestrian Right-of-Way Maintenance"), DPW
is responsible for the repair of sidewalk defects on public
right-of-way, including cracked, heaved, or uneven concrete panels,
provided the defect is not caused by an adjacent private property
owner's tree roots (see Section 4, cost-recovery provisions).

2. SCOPE

Applies to pedestrian sidewalks only. Road-surface defects (potholes,
street pavement) are DOT's responsibility under DOT Road Maintenance SOP
v2 — do not confuse the two even though both involve "pavement." Curb
ramps and ADA-accessibility defects are covered under this policy as an
extension of standard sidewalk maintenance.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  TRIP HAZARD (vertical displacement greater than 3/4 inch, or a
       report explicitly describing an injury/fall): Inspected within 2
       business days. If confirmed, a temporary hazard marking (paint or
       cone) is placed same-day, with permanent repair scheduled within
       15 business days.

  3.2  NON-HAZARD DEFECT (cracking, minor unevenness without trip risk):
       Inspected within 10 business days, repair scheduled within 30
       business days as part of the quarterly sidewalk repair batch.

4. COST RECOVERY

Where a sidewalk defect is caused by a tree root from a tree on adjacent
private property, DPW may issue a cost-recovery notice to the property
owner under City Code §16-5(c). This does not delay the repair itself.

5. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOT Road Maintenance SOP v2 (road surface, not sidewalk), and
DHUD Public Housing Maintenance SOP for sidewalks located on public
housing authority property, which fall under DHUD's separate maintenance
process rather than this policy.
