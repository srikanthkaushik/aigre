DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
General Citizen Intake FAQ
Last updated: July 2026

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: What kinds of issues does DPW handle?

A: Sidewalks, water and sewer mains, street lighting, trash and recycling
collection, snow removal, storm drains, right-of-way trees (urban
forestry), graffiti on public infrastructure, bulk-item pickup scheduling,
dead-animal removal, and DPW-managed public buildings (city hall,
libraries, community centers).

Q: What does DPW NOT handle?

A: Road surface/potholes (DOT), traffic signals and signage (DOT),
illegal dumping (DEP), water quality/contamination testing (DEP), school
buildings (DOE), and public housing buildings (DHUD).

Q: I'm not sure if my issue is DPW or DOT — what should I do?

A: Submit it anyway with as much detail as you can (exact location, what
you're seeing). Misrouted reports are corrected by department staff
during triage; you don't need to know the exact department in advance.

Q: How do I know if a report I filed is a duplicate of one already
filed?

A: You don't need to check — the system automatically links duplicate
reports of the same issue at the same location and applies the original
report's timeline rather than opening a redundant case.
