Trash & Recycling Collection SOP

STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DPW's response to reports of missed scheduled trash or recycling
collection. [[XREF]]This SOP does NOT cover illegal dumping on public or private
property (see DEP Illegal Dumping Enforcement Policy) — a missed
collection is a scheduling/operations issue involving material the
citizen properly set out for pickup; illegal dumping is unauthorized
disposal by a third party.[[/XREF]]

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  MISSED COLLECTION, single address: Logged and routed to the
       collection contractor for makeup pickup within 2 business days of
       report.

  2.2  MISSED COLLECTION, entire route/block (5 or more reports from the
       same route on the same day): Treated as a route-level service
       failure; makeup collection for the full route scheduled within 1
       business day.

  2.3  DAMAGED OR MISSING CITY-ISSUED BIN: Replacement bin delivered
       within 10 business days.

3. DISTINGUISHING FROM ILLEGAL DUMPING

[[XREF]]If the citizen report describes debris, furniture, or material that was
NOT properly set out on a scheduled collection day (e.g., dumped in an
alley, vacant lot, or someone else's property), route to DEP under the
Illegal Dumping Enforcement Policy instead of logging as a missed
collection.[[/XREF]]

4. RELATED POLICIES

See also DEP Illegal Dumping Enforcement Policy, and DPW Citizen FAQ —
Public Works Services for bulk-item pickup scheduling (a separate
process from routine trash/recycling).
