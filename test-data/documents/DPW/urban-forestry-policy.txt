CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Urban Forestry Policy
Document No. DPW-POL-018           Effective Date: March 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DPW's maintenance responsibility for trees in the public
right-of-way (between the sidewalk and the street, or in a designated
tree lawn/median). Expands on the brief mention in DPW Citizen FAQ —
Public Works Services.

2. SCOPE

  2.1  RIGHT-OF-WAY TREES: DPW Urban Forestry is responsible for pruning,
       storm-damage cleanup, and removal of city-owned trees.

  2.2  DISEASED OR HAZARDOUS RIGHT-OF-WAY TREE: If a citizen reports a
       tree that appears diseased, dead, or structurally unsound
       (visible cracking, leaning), DPW dispatches an arborist assessment
       within 10 business days; if confirmed hazardous, removal is
       scheduled on an expedited basis.

  2.3  PROTECTED/HERITAGE TREES: [[XREF]]Some right-of-way and even some private-
       property trees are designated "protected" under the separate DEP
       Tree Preservation Ordinance Policy — removal of a protected tree,
       even a hazardous one, requires DEP sign-off in addition to DPW's
       assessment.[[/XREF]] This is a genuine two-department case, not a routing
       error: DPW assesses the hazard, DEP confirms protected status.

  2.4  STORM DAMAGE: Fallen branches or downed right-of-way trees
       following a storm are prioritized same-day if blocking a street or
       sidewalk, next-business-day otherwise.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DEP Tree Preservation Ordinance Policy and DPW Sidewalk
Maintenance Policy (for sidewalk damage caused by root heaving).
