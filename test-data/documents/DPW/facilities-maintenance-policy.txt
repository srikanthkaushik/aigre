CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
DPW-Managed Public Facilities Maintenance Policy
Document No. DPW-POL-021           Effective Date: November 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs maintenance of public buildings under DPW's direct management —
city hall, public libraries, community centers, and recreation facilities.
Distinct from DOE School Facilities Maintenance SOP (school buildings are
DOE's responsibility, not DPW's, even though both are "public buildings")
and distinct from DHUD Public Housing Maintenance SOP (public housing
buildings are DHUD's, not DPW's).

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  HVAC OR STRUCTURAL HAZARD in an occupied DPW-managed facility:
       Same standard as DOE's — HIGH priority for HVAC affecting occupied
       space, CRITICAL for structural hazards, per the Citywide
       Escalation & Safety-Flag Policy.

  2.2  ROUTINE MAINTENANCE (fixtures, painting, minor repairs): MEDIUM
       priority, 5-10 business days depending on scope.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DOE School Facilities Maintenance SOP and DHUD Public Housing
Maintenance SOP — three separate departments each own a distinct category
of "public building," a common source of citizen confusion.
