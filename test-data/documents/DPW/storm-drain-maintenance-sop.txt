CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Storm Drain & Catch Basin Maintenance SOP
Document No. DPW-SOP-037           Effective Date: September 1, 2024
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DPW's response to blocked or overflowing storm drains and catch
basins, distinct from the sanitary sewer system covered under DPW Water
Main Break Response SOP.

2. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  2.1  ACTIVE STREET FLOODING due to a blocked catch basin during or
       immediately after rainfall: HIGH priority, crew dispatched within
       2 hours to clear the blockage.

  2.2  NON-EMERGENCY BLOCKED DRAIN (debris visible, no active flooding):
       MEDIUM priority, cleared within 5 business days as part of the
       routine maintenance sweep.

  2.3  [[XREF]]Reports of water quality concerns (discoloration, odor) at a storm
       drain outfall are NOT covered by this SOP — route to DEP under the
       Wetlands & Stormwater Runoff Policy, since that concerns
       contamination rather than a mechanical blockage.[[/XREF]]

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DPW Water Main Break Response SOP (sanitary/potable water, a
different system) and DEP Wetlands & Stormwater Runoff Policy.
