CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Snow Removal Priority Routes Policy
Policy 7.3                          Effective Date: October 1, 2023
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Establishes the tiered priority system for snow and ice removal on
city-maintained streets during winter weather events.

2. PRIORITY TIERS

  TIER 1 — Emergency routes: streets providing access to hospitals, fire
  stations, and the primary bus network. Plowed continuously during an
  active snow event and within 4 hours of snowfall ending.

  TIER 2 — Collector streets and school-zone approach streets: plowed
  within 8 hours of snowfall ending.

  TIER 3 — Residential side streets: plowed within 24 hours of snowfall
  ending, weather and crew availability permitting.

3. CITIZEN REPORTS

A citizen report that "my street hasn't been plowed" is checked against
the street's assigned tier before any escalation. A Tier 3 street 12
hours after snowfall ends is within policy and does not warrant
escalation; a Tier 1 or Tier 2 street outside its window is escalated to
the on-duty Snow Operations supervisor same-day.

4. RELATED POLICIES

This policy governs snow/ice conditions specifically. Underlying road
surface defects (potholes revealed after snowmelt) are governed by DOT
Road Maintenance SOP v2, not this policy.
