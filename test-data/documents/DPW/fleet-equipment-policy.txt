CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Fleet & Equipment Maintenance Policy (Internal)
Document No. DPW-INT-004           Effective Date: January 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT — INTERNAL DOCUMENT, not citizen-facing

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Internal policy governing maintenance scheduling for DPW's vehicle fleet
(plow trucks, sweepers, collection trucks) and field equipment. Referenced
here because fleet downtime can affect citizen-facing SLAs (e.g., a
sweeper truck out of service can delay a bike-lane debris response) and
staff should be aware of the connection when explaining a delay to a
citizen.

2. MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

  2.1  Plow trucks undergo mandatory pre-season inspection each September;
       any truck failing inspection is pulled from the winter rotation
       until repaired, which can reduce available capacity during a
       storm.

  2.2  Routine fleet maintenance is scheduled to avoid overlapping with
       peak-demand periods (leaf season for street sweepers, winter for
       plows) wherever possible.

3. CITIZEN-FACING NOTE

If a citizen asks why a response is delayed, staff should NOT cite
internal fleet issues as a blanket excuse without checking whether the
delay genuinely falls outside the applicable SLA — most delays are still
within SLA tolerances even during high-demand periods.
