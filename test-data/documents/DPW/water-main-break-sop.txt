CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Water Main Break Response SOP
Document No. DPW-SOP-021           Effective Date: May 1, 2023
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DPW's response to reports of city-owned water main breaks,
distinct from in-building plumbing or utility issues within a private or
publicly-subsidized rental unit (see DHUD Tenant Utility Complaint Guide
for the latter — DPW does NOT dispatch for loss of water pressure inside
an individual apartment unless a main break is independently confirmed to
be the cause).

2. SCOPE

Applies to breaks, leaks, or pressure loss affecting a city water main
under a public street or right-of-way. A single citizen report of "no
water" in one building, absent corroborating reports from neighboring
addresses, is treated as a potential in-building issue and referred per
Section 4 rather than dispatched as a main-break case.

3. RESPONSE TIMELINE

  3.1  CONFIRMED ACTIVE BREAK (visible surface water, multiple
       corroborating reports from a street segment): Emergency crew
       dispatched within 1 hour. Initial containment (shutoff valve
       isolation) target: 2 hours from confirmation. Full repair target:
       24 hours from confirmation, per the Citywide SLA Policy Summary's
       HIGH-priority resolve window.

  3.2  SUSPECTED BREAK (single report, no visible surface water): Field
       crew dispatched within 4 hours to confirm or rule out.

4. REFERRAL FOR IN-UNIT ISSUES

If a citizen reports loss of water pressure or hot water inside their own
unit and DPW's street-level pressure readings are normal, the citizen is
referred to DHUD's tenant complaint process (for subsidized/public
housing) or advised that in-unit plumbing is the property owner's
responsibility.

5. RELATED POLICIES

See also DHUD Tenant Utility Complaint Guide, and Citywide Escalation &
Safety-Flag Policy for the hazard criteria that apply to any water main
break located near an electrical hazard (e.g., a submerged live wire).
