CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Animal Control & Removal SOP
Document No. DPW-SOP-035           Effective Date: April 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs DPW's removal of deceased animals from public streets and
right-of-way, and coordination with Animal Control for live-animal
concerns (which are handled by a separate city Animal Control unit, not
DPW directly).

2. SCOPE

  2.1  DECEASED ANIMAL ON A PUBLIC STREET: DPW dispatches for removal —
       same-day if reported before noon, next business day otherwise.

  2.2  DECEASED ANIMAL ON PRIVATE PROPERTY: Not a DPW responsibility; the
       property owner is responsible for disposal.

  2.3  LIVE ANIMAL CONCERNS (stray, injured, aggressive): Referred to
       Animal Control, a separate city unit with its own dispatch line —
       the grievance portal logs the report and forwards it, but Animal
       Control's response timelines are not governed by this SOP or the
       standard citywide SLA table.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DPW Trash & Recycling Collection SOP (unrelated process,
sometimes confused since both involve "things on the street").
