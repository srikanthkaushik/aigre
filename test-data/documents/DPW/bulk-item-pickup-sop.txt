CITY DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS
Bulk-Item Pickup SOP
Document No. DPW-SOP-033           Effective Date: February 1, 2025
STATUS: CURRENT

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE

Governs the scheduled bulk-item pickup program (furniture, appliances,
mattresses) — a citizen-requested SCHEDULING process, distinct from
routine weekly trash/recycling collection under DPW Trash & Recycling
Collection SOP, and distinct from unauthorized dumping under DEP Illegal
Dumping Enforcement Policy.

2. PROCESS

  2.1  Citizens request a pickup date through the portal, at least 5
       business days in advance. This is a scheduling request, not a
       complaint — it has no SLA in the sense of "time to resolve a
       problem," only a minimum lead time.

  2.2  Up to 3 bulk items per scheduled pickup, placed curbside the night
       before the scheduled date.

  2.3  MISSED SCHEDULED BULK PICKUP (citizen scheduled correctly, items
       were out, but not collected): This IS a service-failure complaint
       with a standard MEDIUM-priority SLA — makeup pickup within 3
       business days.

  2.4  ITEMS LEFT OUT WITHOUT A SCHEDULED PICKUP: Not a DPW bulk-pickup
       matter — if left on someone else's property or a public space
       without authorization, this is a DEP illegal-dumping matter
       instead.

3. RELATED POLICIES

See also DPW Trash & Recycling Collection SOP and DEP Illegal Dumping
Enforcement Policy.
