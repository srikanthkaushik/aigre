PART Saf -C 6202 CALCULATION OF ACTUAL EXPENSES

Saf -C 6202.01 Calculation of Actual Expenses .

(a) Actual expenses incurred shall be calculated by the sum of the following items when applicable:

(1) The cost of personnel shall be calculated by the total number of hours required by the course curriculum multiplied by the average hourly rates of division employees anticipated to be involved in the training or service;

(2) The cost of mileage reimbursement shall be calculated by the average number of miles reimbursed to the division employees anticipated to be involved in the training or service multiplied by the state approved mileage reimbursed rate outlined in the NH department of administrative services Manual of Procedures MOP 1300 available at http://sunspot.nh.gov/ DASManualProcedures /Documents/MOP/MOP%201300.doc;

(3) The cost of books and manuals shall be the sum of the costs of the books and manuals that are required by the course curriculum for each student of the training;

(4) The cost of mailing shall be calculated to include all envelopes, certification paper, and postage costs anticipated to be needed as part of the training or service;

(5) The cost of equipment shall be the purchase price of any equipment that is required to be purchased based on the training or service;

(6) The cost of consumables shall be calculated by the current cost of such consumables in the Concord, New Hampshire area multiplied by the quantity that is required for the training or service; and

(7) The cost of certification expenses shall be the estimated number of certifications issued for the training multiplied by the cost of certification with the accreditation body.

(b) Under all circumstances all expenses shall be incurred following official State of New Hampshire purchasing requirements outlined in the NH department of administrative services Manual of Procedures MOP 1600, available at http://sunspot.nh.gov/DASManualProcedures/Documents/ MOP/ MOP%201600.doc.

PART Saf -C 6203 TUITION

Saf -C 6203.01 Tuition Fees .

(a) The tuition for a program or course conducted by the division as training shall be published on the division website at https://www.nh.gov/safety/divisions/fstems/documents/CourseCosts.pdf.

(b) The tuition fees for Firefighter I certification courses given to New Hampshire emergency service personnel shall be the per capita share of the actual expenses incurred by the division, as calculated per Saf -C 6202, less 95%.

(c) The tuition fees for Firefighter II certification courses given to New Hampshire emergency service personnel shall be the per capita share of the actual expenses incurred by the division, as calculated per Saf -C 6202, less 90%.

(d) The tuition fees for certification courses or programs, excluding those outlined in (b) and (c) above, given to New Hampshire emergency service personnel shall be the per capita share of the actual expenses incurred by the division, as calculated per Saf -C 6202, less 65%.

(e) The tuition fees for non-certification courses or programs conducted by the division and given to New Hampshire emergency service personnel shall be the per capita share of the actual expenses incurred as calculated per Saf -C 6202.

(f) Any courses offered as a result of the awarding of a grant shall have fees assessed in accordance with the requirements set forth in the grant.

(g) For emergency service personnel from another state or non-emergency service personnel, the tuition fees for any course or program conducted shall be the per capita share of the actual expenses incurred by the division, as calculated per Saf -C 6202, plus 45%.

PART Saf -C 6204 EXAMINATIONS

Saf -C 6204.01 Certification Examination Fees .

(a) The division shall charge a $30.00 fee for any retest given to a student who has failed a written certification examination or single station practical examination.

(b) The division shall charge a $75.00 fee for any retest given to a student who has failed the entire practical examination.

(c) Any student who fails to appear for a retest of a certification examination shall be charged a $20.00 fee, unless notification is given at least 5 hours prior to the examination.

(d) The division shall charge actual expenses incurred to any organization which completes a certification course that is not coordinated or funded by the division.

Saf -C 6204.02 Reciprocity Examination Fees .

(a) Students seeking certification based upon training taken in other jurisdictions shall be charged:

(1) A $100.00 fee for Firefighter I certification, including all components;

(2) A $80.00 fee for Firefighter II certification;

(3) A $60.00 fee for all other certifications; and

(4) A $400.00 fee for emergency service personnel from another state or non-emergency service personnel seeking New Hampshire certification, requiring research.

PART Saf -C 6205 UTILIZATION OF FIRE ACADEMY FACILITY AND MOBILE PROPS

Saf -C 6205.01 Fire Academy Facility Fees . Fees for utilization of the fire academy facilities shall be as follows:

(a) Use of the auditorium shall be charged at the rate of $175 per day or $125 per half- day;

(b) Use of a double classroom shall be charged at the rate of $150 per day or $75 per half-day; and

(c) Use of a single classroom shall be charged at the rate of $100 per day or $50 per half-day.

Source. #7552, INTERIM, eff 9-5-01, EXPIRED: 3-4-02

New. #7747, eff 8-17-02; ss by #9496, eff 6-26-09; ss by #12477, eff 2-8-18 (formerly Saf -C 6204.01)

Saf -C 6205.02 Mobile Prop Fees .

(a) New Hampshire fire department and fire-related state agencies shall be charged fees for the utilization of mobile props, as follows:

(1) There shall be no charge for the first 2 utilizations of mobile props in a fiscal year; and

(2) Each subsequent utilization of mobile props in a fiscal year shall be the actual expenses incurred by the division, as calculated per Saf -C 6202 .

(b) Individuals and entities that are not New Hampshire fire departments and fire-related state agencies shall be charged the actual expenses incurred by the division for utilization of mobile props.

Source. #12477, eff 2-8-18 (formerly Saf -C 6204.02)

PART Saf -C 6206 INDUSTRIAL TRAINING

Saf -C 6206.01 Industrial Training Fees .

(a) The fees for industrial training shall be the actual expenses incurred by the division, as calculated per Saf -C 6202, plus 75-100%, based upon the complexity of the training.

(b) The fees for training to state agencies shall be the actual expenses accrued to the division, plus 10%.

PART Saf -C 6209 WAIVERS

Saf -C 6209.01 Waiver of Fees .

(a) Upon written request, the commissioner shall waive fees charged to a governmental entity or non-profit organization for good cause, consisting of the following:

(1) The fees requested to be waived shall consist only of fees charged for utilization of the fire academy facility;

(2) The governmental entity or non-profit organization shall request to utilize the fire academy facility during normal business hours at a time when the facility is available; and

(3) The governmental entity or non-profit organization shall not have had fire academy facility utilization fees waived in the previous 6 months.

(b) The governmental entity or non-profit organization shall be notified in writing whether the requested fee waiver has been granted, and if the request is denied, the commissioner s decision shall be final.

Source. #12477, eff 2-8-18 (formerly Saf -C 6208.01)
