CHAPTER Saf-C 1700 INTERNATIONAL REGISTRATION PLAN RULES

REVISION NOTE:

Document #13379, effective 5-23-22, readopted with amendment Chapter Saf-C 1700 titled International Registration Plan Rules , which contained Part Saf-C 1701 through Saf-C 1703 pursuant to RSA 260:73, as amended by 2019, 98:2, effective 6-21-19, which exempts the rules from RSA 541-A.

Chapter Saf-C 1700 had previously been adopted by Document #10136, effective 5-23-12, which had contained Part Saf-C 1701 through Part Saf-C 1713 pursuant to RSA 260:74 and RSA 260:75, when the rules were subject to RSA 541-A. RSA 260:74 and RSA 260:75 were subsequently repealed by 2019, 98:3, I and II, effective 6-21-19. Chapter Saf-C 1700 in Document #13379 then superseded the former Chapter Saf-C 1700 before the rules filed under Document #10136 could expire on 5-23-22.

Document #13379 extensively amended the rules filed in Document #10136 and replaced all prior filings affecting rules in the former Chapter Saf-C 1700. The prior filings included the following documents:

PART Saf-C 1703 DISPUTES REGARDING FEES AND CHARGES

Saf-C 1703.01 Hearings on Fees and Charges . Any person who wishes to dispute fees or charges against him or her by the state under this subdivision shall be entitled to a hearing before the commissioner or his or her designee in accordance with the hearing process established by RSA 260:44, II, which shall be liberally construed so as to also apply to this subdivision.
